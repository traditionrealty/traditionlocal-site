Yogurtland in Memorial City Mall offers a reliably sweet stop for those craving creamy frozen yogurt with fresh, colorful toppings. Despite being tucked inside a busy mall, this location keeps things clean, efficient, and friendly—especially with a manager who takes pride in maintaining quality ingredients and crisp presentation. With rotating seasonal flavors and classic staples, this shop remains a go-to treat spot for shoppers and families.

### **What You’ll Love**

- **Fresh toppings only** — no frozen fruit, and it shows in the taste
- **New seasonal flavors** like pumpkin spice cold brew & milk tea
- **Classic staples** such as Dutch chocolate and pistachio
- **Clean machines & tidy space** with smart sanitary updates
- **Perfect for a quick treat while strolling the mall**

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Oct 25, 2021):**

“Solid location of Yogurtland. Although located in the mall, this spot offers plenty of flavors that are delectable with fresh toppings. The manager was kind and passionate, explaining they only use fresh fruit—never frozen. The strawberries and blueberries had a great bite and vivid color.

I tried the pumpkin spice cold brew coffee and milk tea yogurt flavors today—both were great. I love that Yogurtland often introduces new flavors each season. Dutch chocolate and pistachio remain classics: crisp, creamy, and always reliable.

I actually think it’s best that they halted the usage of sample cups—those were becoming unsanitary. This location is clean, and so are the yogurt machines. They no longer have the water fountain at the front, but honestly, precaution measures are appreciated.

Yogurtland is a sweet spot to drop by for a cup of yogurt before strolling around the mall. I’ll definitely be back to try new flavors. Until next time!”
