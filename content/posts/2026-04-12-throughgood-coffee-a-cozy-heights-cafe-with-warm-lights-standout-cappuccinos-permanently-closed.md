Tucked into a quiet residential pocket of **The Heights in Houston**, Throughgood Coffee is the kind of neighborhood café you almost miss if you’re driving by — and that’s part of its charm. Small from the outside and effortlessly cozy within, this local coffee shop offers a warm, inviting atmosphere perfect for morning strolls, casual meetups, or a solo caffeine break.

Known for its thoughtfully crafted espresso drinks, tempting pastries, and relaxed neighborhood vibe, **Throughgood Coffee has become a go-to Heights coffee shop** for those seeking quality over flash. With glowing Edison-style bulbs overhead and a taco truck parked just outside, the experience feels both intimate and uniquely Houston.

## The Atmosphere

From the outside, Throughgood Coffee feels understated — almost hidden in its quaint neighborhood setting. But step inside, and you’re greeted by warm lighting, soft ambiance, and a welcoming café energy that makes you want to linger.

The café is compact, giving it an intimate feel rather than a bustling coffeehouse vibe. The glowing lightbulbs create a comforting atmosphere that pairs perfectly with a hot espresso drink on a cool Houston morning.

Bonus: There’s a taco truck nestled just outside, adding a casual, community-driven touch. (Still on the list to try next time.)

## What We Ordered

### ☕ Cappuccino with ½ Pump Vanilla

The cappuccino was warm, decadent, and beautifully balanced. Adding half a pump of vanilla enhanced the flavor without overpowering the espresso — just enough sweetness to round out the drink.

What stood out most was the foam. Too often, cappuccinos fall flat with minimal volume or underwhelming texture. This one delivered — airy yet structured foam with solid volume, especially for a smaller-sized drink.

**Flavor:** Smooth, lightly sweet, well-balanced

**Foam:** Full-bodied and properly textured

**Verdict:** One of the better cappuccinos in The Heights
