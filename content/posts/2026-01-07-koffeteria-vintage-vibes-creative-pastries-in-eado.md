Koffeteria brings an artistic, vintage charm to EaDo—a neighborhood known for its creative energy and urban edge. With its antique cupboards, quirky décor, and inventive pastry case, this café stands out as a destination for those who love personality in their coffee shops. Whether you're stopping in before exploring downtown or grabbing a treat on the go, Koffeteria offers a mix of bold flavors and nostalgic ambiance.

While service can fluctuate depending on the time of day, the café still draws visitors for its refreshing beverages, imaginative baked goods, and distinctive, old-world interior. It's a spot worth returning to, especially earlier in the day, to catch their most famous pastries at their freshest.

**Must-Try Items:**

- Iced Latte (refreshing, milky, lightly balanced)
- Herbal Iced Tea (flavorful + lightly sweetened)
- Thick & Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookie
- Eclectic Croissants (not yet tried, but highly talked about!)

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jun 5, 2022):** “Unique and vintage coffee shoppe within the EaDo district of Houston. This part of town isn't too far off from the novelty of hanging out, or heading towards downtown htx. Just dropped by for a little bit as I was headed towards discovery green for a venture in the park.

My friend and I entered about 30 minutes before closing. The staff was as stiff as cardboard. No warmth or anything. We decided upon cold bevvies as it was a warm day out in the city. They made me a simple iced latte, which was refreshing and milky. Not too strong. Just good. My friend enjoyed his herbal iced tea which was packed with flavor and was lightly sweetened.

We also grabbed their thick and chewy chocolate chip cookie. It was delicious and buttery. Not entirely sure how the other pastries would fare but they certainly looked tasty.

I loved the antique interior—the cupboard, cabinets, and little trinkets. The coffee table was dirty though and so was the floor around where we sat. Staff might have gotten busy since they were 30 mins til closing.

A little hyped. May visit earlier next time around and grab a different bevvie. Have heard quite a lot about their eclectic croissants. I'm not usually in the EaDo district of town, as it's a bit ways out. Definitely an urban exploration, but will certainly revisit when given the chance.”
