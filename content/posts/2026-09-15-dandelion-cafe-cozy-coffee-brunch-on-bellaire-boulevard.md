Nestled along **Bellaire Boulevard in Bellaire, TX**, Dandelion Cafe brings cozy charm and locally sourced coffee to a part of town that could certainly use more independent cafés. With inviting décor, friendly service, and a menu that spans coffee, smoothies, breakfast plates, and light bites, it’s an appealing neighborhood hangout.

Though I live in Sugar Land, supporting local businesses across Houston is always worth the drive, especially when the coffee program is strong.

If you’re searching for:

- Best coffee shop in Bellaire TX
- Breakfast and brunch Bellaire Boulevard
- Greenway Coffee Bellaire
- Cozy café near Sugar Land

Dandelion Cafe is a spot to explore.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Cozy, inviting interior
- Warm and friendly service
- Serves locally roasted Greenway Coffee
- Handmade seasonal syrups
- Comfortable hangout for friends and family

## The Coffee

Dandelion Cafe proudly serves **Greenway Coffee**, a respected Houston favorite known for smooth, aromatic beans.

### ☕ Pumpkin Spice & Gingerbread Latte

Both seasonal drinks were enjoyable during the holidays. The handmade syrups added a noticeable depth and authenticity that elevated each cup.

The espresso is smooth, aromatic, and well-balanced, easily the strongest draw of the café.

If there’s one thing that guarantees a return visit, it’s the coffee.

## The Food

The menu includes breakfast plates, sandwiches, bagels, soups, salads, smoothies, and desserts, offering something for everyone.

### 🥪 Veggie Delight Sandwich

Ordered on two separate visits with noticeably different experiences.

**First Visit:**

- Generously layered with marinated eggplant, caramelized onions, red pepper spread, grilled asparagus, and mushrooms
- Served on lighter French-style bread
- Accompanied by a small cup of watermelon
- Flavorful and satisfying

**Second Visit:**

- Smaller portion size
- Darker bread that didn’t pair as well with the roasted vegetables
- Served with pickles instead of fruit
- Less impressive overall

Consistency matters, especially when menu prices lean toward the higher side.

### 🍞 Stuffed French Toast

A highlight. Comforting, sweet, and worth ordering again.

### 🥯 Bagel Dots with Vegetable Cream Cheese

Fun, flavorful, and a solid lighter bite.

## The Atmosphere

Both the exterior and interior are thoughtfully decorated, creating a warm, welcoming space. It’s a great place to gather with friends or relax with a latte.

Simple café vibes, neighborhood energy, and a comfortable environment make it easy to linger.

## Review

**Sara I. (Dec 29, 2017):**

> “I definitely like the concept of this place. Cute and cozy tucked on Bellaire Blvd.The coffee here is great, they use Greenway Coffee. The syrups are handmade and you can taste it.The veggie sandwich was excellent the first time, but much smaller and less impressive the second time.I did enjoy the stuffed French toast and bagel dots.Think I’ll just stick to a cafe latte and something sweet off the menu.”

## Overall Impression

Dandelion Cafe shines brightest with its coffee program and cozy neighborhood ambiance. While food consistency may vary, the Greenway Coffee and handmade syrups keep it firmly on the radar.

For Bellaire locals seeking a relaxed brunch café or anyone craving a smooth latte in a welcoming setting, Dandelion Cafe is worth checking out.
