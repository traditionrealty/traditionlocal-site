If you’ve spent time driving down Westheimer late at night, chances are you’ve spotted **La Super Hueva Tacos y Tortas**, a taco truck parked in a well-lit plaza in **Westchase, Houston**. Formerly known as *El Gato*, this no-frills food truck has quietly built a following for simple, satisfying Mexican street tacos and tortas — especially for those late-night hunger pangs.

With a compact menu featuring **tacos (corn or flour tortillas), fajitas, quesadillas, and classic proteins like beef, barbacoa, and chicken**, La Super Hueva keeps things straightforward. It’s not flashy — but it’s convenient, quick, and hits the spot when fast food just won’t do.

## What to Order

### 🌮 Beef & Barbacoa Tacos

The go-to order usually includes **beef tacos or barbacoa (shredded beef)**, occasionally chicken — served on corn tortillas for that authentic street taco feel.

Each taco is topped with:

- Fresh chopped white onion
- Bright cilantro
- Lime wedge on the side

The tacos are small but surprisingly filling, making it easy to mix and match a few. They aren’t overly greasy, which is a plus for late-night bites.

### 🌶 Green Salsa – Proceed with Caution

Always request the green salsa on the side — but fair warning: **muy caliente**. It brings bold heat and elevates the tacos, but spice-sensitive diners should pace themselves.

## The Experience

La Super Hueva is parked in a lit plaza along Westheimer, surrounded by other restaurants and shops — making it an easy stop while out and about. It’s casual, quick, and designed for grab-and-go dining rather than lingering.

While there’s nothing particularly elevated about the setup, convenience plays a big role here. When hunger strikes late at night, it’s often a better alternative than traditional fast-food chains.

That said, Houston is full of exceptional taco trucks — and while this one is solid, there may be stronger contenders across the city.
