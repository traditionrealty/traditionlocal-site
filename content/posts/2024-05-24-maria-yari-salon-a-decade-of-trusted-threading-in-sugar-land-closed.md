***[PERMANENTLY CLOSED - Store No Longer Operating]*** **... The new salon is also good, it's called** **Rise and Shine Salon.** 

Maria Yari Salon is one of Sugar Land’s quietly legendary beauty stops—small, personal, and fully driven by the skill of one expert: Maria herself. For many loyal clients, she’s the only person they trust with their brows. Her threading style is quick, precise, and remarkably consistent, which is why this tiny studio often fills up during evenings and weekends. Appointments are recommended, but the loyal crowd is proof enough: Maria’s craft is worth chasing.

### **What to Order (Services to Choose)**

- **Eyebrow Threading** – Clean, shaped, and precise every time.
- **Upper Lip Threading** – Fast, tidy, and minimal discomfort.
- **Facial Waxing** – Efficient and handled with care.

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Mar 28, 2019):** 

“I've been gettin' my brows threaded by Maria for well over a decade. Much before she opened up her own salon. Been chasin' her around cause she's really the only one who can touch my face. She's great with all of the services provided; such as eyebrow and upper lip threading, waxing, etc. I can't imagine going anywhere else cause she really has that magic in her hands. Her threading and waxing services are fast and effortless. She's really the only one man handling the salon.

This place can get quite busy depending on the time and day that you visit—specifically towards the evening and weekend. Make sure to book an appointment if you're limited on time. Anyway, I wish that I could shrink Maria into a Polly Pocket when I'm traveling. Cause those brows won't thread themselves. You'll understand if you've got South Asian genetics.

Visit her if you want a clean looking aesthetic face. Tis' all.”
