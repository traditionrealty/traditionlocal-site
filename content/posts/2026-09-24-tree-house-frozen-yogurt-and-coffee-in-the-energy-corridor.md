Tree House is a nice, new, and adorable little spot in the Energy Corridor that combines two favorites under one roof. Coffee and frozen yogurt. That alone makes it worth checking out.

The concept is simple and fun. You can grab a cup of coffee, build your own frozen yogurt creation, or enjoy both if you are feeling indulgent.

If you are searching for:

Tree House is a sweet option.

## Why You Will Like It

- Unique concept combining coffee and frozen yogurt
- Multiple frozen yogurt flavor choices
- Fresh fruit topping bar
- Clean and nicely decorated space
- Friendly service

## The Atmosphere

The café is clean, bright, and thoughtfully decorated. It feels welcoming and relaxed. It is the kind of neighborhood spot you can quickly pop into for a treat before heading to work or meeting friends.

The young lady at the register was friendly and helpful, even offering a stamp card for future visits. Since I do not live close by, I passed on it, but it was a nice touch

## What I Tried

### Frozen Yogurt

There were several flavor options to choose from. I went with cake batter and vanilla.

Both flavors were good, though not the most amazing frozen yogurt I have ever had. What stood out most was the fresh fruit at the topping bar. The fruit was neatly chopped and beautifully displayed, which made customizing the yogurt even more enjoyable.

It was a warm day, and the frozen yogurt was refreshing before heading into work.

## Overall Impression

Tree House is a cute local concept that blends coffee culture with frozen yogurt fun. While the frozen yogurt flavors were solid rather than spectacular, the fresh toppings and welcoming environment make it worth a visit.

Next time, I am looking forward to trying the coffee offerings. It is always nice to explore unique neighborhood spots like this one.
