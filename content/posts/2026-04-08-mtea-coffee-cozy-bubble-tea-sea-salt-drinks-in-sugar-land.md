Sugar Land continues to welcome new café concepts, and **MTea & Coffee** is one of the latest cozy additions to the scene. Located along **Highway Six**, this bubble tea and coffee shop has taken over the former Hippo Ice space — bringing with it a warm, minimal aesthetic and a menu centered around refreshing specialty drinks.

While the official signage was still pending at the time of visit, the café is visibly noticeable and thoughtfully designed inside. With unique seating, soft modern touches, and a relaxed vibe, MTea & Coffee adds another inviting option for those searching for **bubble tea in Sugar Land** or a casual place to unwind with friends.

## The Atmosphere

MTea & Coffee keeps things simple yet inviting. The interior design leans modern and minimal, with unique seating arrangements that make it easy to gather with friends or enjoy a quiet moment alone.

Music was slightly louder than preferred during the visit — a common café trend — but the overall atmosphere remained comfortable. On this particular evening, the café was quiet, though it has all the potential to become a lively community hangout.

Outdoor seating is available, though Houston mosquitos may convince you to stay indoors next time.

## What We Ordered

### 🍉 Fresh Watermelon Juice

Blended smoothly with ice, the watermelon juice was crisp, refreshing, and perfect for a warm Texas evening. Light, hydrating, and naturally sweet — an excellent alternative to heavier milk teas.

### 🍵 Iced Green Tea with Sea Salt Foam

The sea salt foam drinks appear to be one of their specialties. This iced green tea was topped with a creamy, lightly salted foam that balanced sweetness with a savory finish — a popular trend in modern bubble tea shops.

**Flavor profile:** Smooth, slightly sweet, subtly salty

**Perfect for:** Those who enjoy layered, specialty tea drinks
