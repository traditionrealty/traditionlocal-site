If you’re craving a true old-fashioned burger joint experience in the **Galleria/Uptown area of Houston**, Cliff’s Hamburger Grill delivers exactly that. No frills, just fresh, satisfying comfort food.

From the moment you step inside, Cliff’s feels like a nostalgic time capsule. The space has that classic, neighborhood-dive charm, the kind of place that’s been around long enough to earn loyal regulars and weekend crowds.

If you’re searching for:

- Best burgers near the Galleria
- Old fashioned hamburger Houston
- Chicken sandwich Uptown Houston
- Classic burger joint Houston

Cliff’s Hamburger Grill belongs on your list.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Old-school burger joint vibe
- Friendly, welcoming staff
- Large and diverse menu
- Indoor and outdoor seating
- Generous portions

## The Atmosphere

Upon entering, we were greeted warmly, including by a friendly older gentleman who joked that the place might be older than my mom.

The interior carries that nostalgic Americana feel. On a Saturday afternoon, the restaurant was packed, families, friends, and longtime regulars filled both indoor and outdoor seating areas.

It’s clear Cliff’s has built a loyal following.

## What I Ordered

Though Cliff’s is known for its beef burgers, I opted for something lighter that day.

### 🥑 California Chicken Sandwich

As a native California girl, this felt like the right choice and it did not disappoint.

The sandwich featured:

- Juicy marinated chicken breast
- Creamy avocado
- Melted white cheese
- Mayo and tomato
- Soft bun

The flavors were balanced and not overly salty. The portion size was generous, filling enough that half the sandwich satisfied me.

### 🧅 Onion Rings

Tried one from a friend’s plate: crispy, golden, and flavorful. A solid side option.

## Menu Variety

Cliff’s offers a surprisingly diverse menu, including:

- Classic beef burgers
- Chicken sandwiches
- Black bean burger
- Salmon burger
- Crispy fish sandwich
- Breakfast options

There’s something for everyone — whether you’re a traditionalist or looking for alternatives.

## Service

Despite the busy Saturday crowd, service was friendly and kind throughout. The food took a little time to arrive, but the pleasant weather and good company made the wait easy.

Fresh, hot, and crispy. The food arrived just right.

## Overall Impression

Cliff’s Hamburger Grill captures that rare old-school burger joint magic, welcoming service, generous portions, and a menu that goes beyond just beef patties.

Whether you’re grabbing a classic burger, trying the California chicken sandwich, or exploring alternative options like the black bean or salmon burger, Cliff’s delivers comfort food with heart.

It’s the kind of place you bring friends and family and leave already planning your next visit.
