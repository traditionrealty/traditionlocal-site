Located in the lively Fountains Plaza in Stafford, BAMBU offers a refreshing lineup of Vietnamese inspired beverages, bubble teas, smoothies, and specialty drinks. While there are several BAMBU locations throughout Houston, this particular spot stands out for being clean, calm, and less hectic than some of the busier branches.

It is a great place to cool down during Houston’s warmer months with something sweet, creamy, and refreshing.

## Why You Will Love It

- Unique coconut cream tea with real coconut pieces
- Authentic Vietnamese beverages with jellies and fruit
- Wide selection of smoothies, teas, and coffees
- Reasonable prices
- Clean and vibrant shopping center location

## The Atmosphere

This BAMBU location is tucked inside Fountains Plaza in Stafford. Compared to other Houston locations, this one feels quieter and more relaxed.

The space is clean and simple, making it a comfortable stop for a quick drink. It is especially enjoyable during warm weather when you want something cold and satisfying.

## What I Recommend

### Coconut Cream Tea with Boba

My favorite drink here.

It is unlike a typical coconut milk tea. The coconut cream blended with actual coconut pieces elevates the flavor and texture. The drink has a thicker viscosity, and the fragrant notes of real coconut are noticeable in every sip.

It is rich, refreshing, and different from the usual bubble tea options.

## Other Menu Highlights

BAMBU is known for its authentic Vietnamese style beverages that often include jellies, fresh fruit, basil seeds, lychee, avocado, and other interesting flavors.

They also offer:

- Smoothies
- Coffee drinks
- Traditional milk teas
- Specialty fruit teas

There is truly something for everyone.

## Overall Impression

BAMBU in Stafford is a solid go to when craving something cold and flavorful. With a wide variety of unique drinks and reasonable pricing, it is a dependable option in the Fountains Plaza area.

If you are unsure what to order, the coconut cream tea with boba is a safe and delicious place to start.
