## *A Sweet Afternoon, Rich, & Made-From-Scratch Pies*

### Review:

##### Sara I. - 2/13/17

"Suhweet! Literally. We enjoyed both pies, deep, rich, and satisfying. They use fresh ingredients such as real chocolate and butter. No lard. Yay! True pies that are proudly made and proudly satisfying."

Sara's Takeaway This was a simple, sweet afternoon that turned into a genuine treat. I brought my sister and mom along to Proud Pie's Katy shop for dessert, and the stop was better stocked than expected, with far more variety than a handful of flavors: multiple pies and a few drink options, all made from real ingredients. It's the kind of stop that turns a normal afternoon into something worth remembering, and with Proud Pie's pies also served at Dish Society restaurants across Greater Houston, it's easy to get a slice even when you can't make it out to Katy."

## Favorite Moment

The fudge pie was the standout bite of the day. Deep, decadent, and intensely chocolatey, it was rich without ever feeling heavy, a must-order for anyone who takes their chocolate seriously.

## Best Bite

Pairing The chocolate silk cream pie paired perfectly with a strong iced coffee. The pie brought smooth, velvety layers and a crisp crust, while the iced coffee balanced out the sweetness on a warm Texas afternoon.

## Review Callout

> "True pies that are proudly made and proudly satisfying."

## Final Impression

Sara's visit shows Proud Pie as a strong pick for anyone craving scratch-made dessert in the Katy area. Real chocolate, real butter, no lard: the quality shows in both texture and flavor. It's an easy stop for a sweet treat with family, and with pies also available at Dish Society restaurants across Greater Houston, there's more than one way to get your hands on a slice.
