Sugar Land just got a little sweeter. With the arrival of **Sharetea**, one of the most recognized international bubble tea brands, the neighborhood now has a reliable go-to for crisp milk teas and chewy tapioca pearls.

Located in a convenient Sugar Land plaza, this modern tea shop offers a clean, well-lit space with plenty of seating — perfect for late-night cravings or casual meetups.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Consistent, quality tapioca pearls
- Customizable sweetness and ice levels
- Clean, modern interior
- Friendly staff
- Quick service

## The Atmosphere

We stopped by around **9:30 PM on New Year’s Eve**, and to our delight, they were open. The lighting inside is bright and inviting, with a cool, minimal vibe and plenty of tables and chairs.

It’s a comfortable space to sit and enjoy your drink rather than rushing out the door.

## What We Ordered

### Tapioca Milk Tea (80% Sweet, Medium Ice)

My go-to order.

- Crisp and refreshing
- Not overly sweet
- Pearls were perfectly chewy
- Balanced milk-to-tea ratio

The ability to adjust sweetness levels is always appreciated. At 80%, it was just right.

### 🍫 Cocoa Milk

My sister ordered the cocoa milk and enjoyed it — creamy and satisfying without being overpowering.

## Menu Notes

One thing I appreciate about this Sharetea location is the straightforward menu. They keep it simple and focused — offering their core tea selections without overwhelming customization.

If you’re looking for Thai tea or almond milk tea, you’ll find other options nearby. Sharetea sticks to what it does best.

## Service

There were no long lines during our visit, and drinks came out quickly. Staff were friendly and even handed out new stamp cards.

Note: Stamp cards from other locations may not transfer due to different ownership.
