Tucked beneath a canopy of trees along Grogan’s Drive, **Third Gen Coffee** feels like a cozy escape—a quiet, woodsy nook with nostalgic winter charm and the warmth of a true neighborhood café. From the moment you step inside, its character shines through: soft lighting, inviting décor, and the unexpected charm of a staircase that leads to a peaceful second-floor lounge.

Upstairs, guests find couches, tables, and charging outlets, making it an ideal setting for studying, reading, or lingering with a warm drink. Seasonal beverages bring extra joy, and the standout this visit was a hot eggnog latte—creamy, festive, and deliciously comforting.

Friendly service and thoughtfully crafted drinks round out the experience, making Third Gen Coffee a must-visit for those seeking a serene moment in The Woodlands. Whether you’re passing through or planning a cozy café day, this corner shop offers warmth, charm, and the kind of atmosphere that invites you to return again and again.

**What to Try:**

- Hot eggnog latte (seasonal favorite)
- Seasonal specialty drinks
- Classic espresso beverages

**Atmosphere Highlights:**

- Woodsy surroundings with crisp air
- Upstairs seating with outlets
- Cozy, warm, and inviting aesthetic
- Friendly, attentive baristas

## **Review**

Sara I. (Jan 2, 2023): 

“Such a great little coffee shoppe tucked in the Woodlands on Grograns drive. I'm diggin' this quiet little corner, cluttered with trees and a few stores lined up together. The air felt & smelled crisp and it totally was nostalgic of some sort of northern winter. Wanting to visit the local bookshop next time.

What I liked most about this coffee shoppe? It's charm and characteristic. That's the first thing I noticed upon stepping in. A bit of light and warmth all around, and a staircase that leads you to the second floor. Ample seating upstairs with a few tables, couch and charging outlets.

They have a few unique flavoured drinks this time of the year. My favorite being the eggnog latte.. but make it hot. I look forward to revisiting and trying different items off the menu, in particular, their seasonal products.

It was a great local experience at third gen coffee, the staff was friendly and the drinks were crafted with love. I totally look forward to revisiting if ever in the area.”
