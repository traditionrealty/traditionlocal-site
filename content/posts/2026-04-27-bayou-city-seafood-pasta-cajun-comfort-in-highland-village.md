Tucked into **Highland Village, Houston**, Bayou City Seafood & Pasta serves up a menu centered around **Cajun, Creole, and classic American seafood dishes**. From crawfish boils and crab bisque to hearty pasta platters and sandwiches, this casual neighborhood spot aims to deliver Gulf Coast comfort in a relaxed setting.

On a chilly Monday evening, seafood and soup felt like the perfect pairing — especially when shared among friends gathered around a large table in this intimate, dimly lit space.

Bayou City Seafood & Pasta is a local option worth noting.

## The Atmosphere

From the outside, the restaurant carries a slightly rustic, barbecue-style appearance. Inside, the vibe shifts to casual and cozy, with dim lighting and a relaxed neighborhood feel.

The dining room isn’t overly large, making it easy to spot friends and settle in comfortably. Service was attentive from the start, with warm greetings from both the host and servers.

## What We Ordered

### 🥣 Crab Bisque

With freezing temperatures outside, a bowl of soup was an easy choice. The crab bisque was creamy and hearty, though slightly heavy on salt. The flavor leaned bold — perhaps a bit too strong for my personal preference.

### 🥖 Complimentary Garlic Bread

Warm and comforting, though also noticeably salty.

### 🥬 Spinach & Artichoke Dip

Served bubbling hot with crisp tortilla chips. Creamy, rich, and satisfying — a crowd-pleasing appetizer.

### 🥒 Fried Pickles

Golden, crisp, and fresh out of the fryer. A classic Southern snack done well.

### 🍫 Chocolate Bread Pudding

The true standout of the night. Each of us ordered our own bowl — rich, warm, and perfectly sweet. The chocolate flavor was deep and comforting, with subtle hints of cinnamon throughout.

This dessert easily stole the spotlight and balanced out the savory, salt-forward dishes.

## Menu Highlights

The menu focuses primarily on:

- Crawfish
- Shrimp
- Crab
- Seafood pasta platters
- Sandwiches and burgers
- A few poultry options

Portions are generous, which contributes to the higher price point — typical for fresh seafood in Houston.

## Service

Service was consistently warm and attentive throughout the meal. The staff created a welcoming environment that made for an enjoyable evening with friends.

## Overall Impression

Bayou City Seafood & Pasta offers a cozy Cajun seafood experience in Highland Village, with generous portions and standout desserts. While some dishes leaned salty, the welcoming service and rich chocolate bread pudding left a positive final impression.

If revisiting, the crab cake sandwich or one of the pasta entrées would be next on the list.

For seafood lovers exploring Houston’s Cajun and Creole scene, it’s a spot worth trying at least once.
