Tucked just outside Kirby near Rice Village, Rustika Cafe & Bakery is a cozy neighborhood spot blending Argentine influences with international café favorites. From empanadas and alfajores to muffins, crepes, and specialty coffee drinks, the menu offers something for nearly every sweet tooth.

Whether you are stopping in for coffee or browsing the pastry case, Rustika delivers a warm and inviting experience in the heart of West University.

Rustika Cafe & Bakery is worth a visit.

## Why You Will Love It

- Argentine style pastries and desserts
- Baked empanadas, not fried

## The Atmosphere

Rustika feels like a quiet neighborhood café. When I visited on a Saturday afternoon, it was not overly crowded, which made for a relaxed experience.

The space is warm and casual, perfect for grabbing coffee while passing through the inner loop. The pastry case offers a colorful display of desserts ranging from traditional Argentine treats to more classic bakery staples.

## What I Tried

### Coffee

The coffee was stellar. Warm, well roasted, and wholesome with a comforting depth of flavor. It paired beautifully with the pastry I ordered.

### Apple Empanada

I asked for it toasted, and it came out fresh, soft, and slightly doughy in the best way. The manager mentioned that all empanadas are baked rather than fried, which keeps them lighter.

The apple filling reminded me of apple pie, with small chunks of apples coated in a fragrant, spiced sauce.

## Overall Impression

Rustika Cafe & Bakery offers a pleasant mix of Argentine specialties and international café favorites. While some items feel a bit pricey and card purchases under ten dollars require a minimum, the quality of the coffee and pastries makes it a solid neighborhood stop.

I am especially looking forward to trying the alfajores next time. Those shortbread cookies filled with dulce de leche are calling my name.

## Review

**Sara I. (Mar 12, 2016):**

Although i'd say this place is more of an international cafe. Their are desserts to please anyone here, ranging from muffins, scones, crepes, etc. I tried the coffee here..and it was stellar. Warm with a roasted taste and wholesome. Had an apple empanada which I asked to be toasted. It was fresh, soft and doughy. I believe I spoke to the manager who was friendly and welcoming. He mentioned that all of the empanadas are baked and not fried. The flavor of the apple empanada reminded me of apple pie, with small chunks of apples clinging to a spiced and fragrant sauce. I give three stars because some of the items are pricey and you'll need a credit card for purchases lower than $10. I'll be visiting soon again to try other items off the menu such as the alfajores (shortbread cookies sandwiched between a layer of dulce de leche [milk caramel]) and the savory items off the menu. Their werent too many people inside when I visited on a Saturday afternoon. Nice and cozy cafe when passing through the inner loop.”
