Amy Hieu Hair Salon is a trusted neighborhood spot in Alief known for its natural approach to hair coloring, warm customer service, and an experienced stylist who genuinely cares about preserving hair health. Amy specializes in natural shades, balayage, ombré, and soft dimensional tones—steering away from harsh bleach and instead leaning into what enhances a client's natural beauty.

The salon experience is calm and attentive. Amy works efficiently with her team, guiding clients through each step with reassurance and professional insight gained from two decades of experience. With family present, a friendly environment, and thoughtful touches like snacks and water, the salon feels both personal and professional.

### **Services & Highlights**

- Natural hair color expertise (no-bleach approach)
- Warm chocolate brown, balayage, and ombré specialties
- Attentive and comforting client care
- Efficient coloring, washing & styling process
- Friendly family-owned atmosphere

### **Review**

**Sara I. (May 31, 2020):** 

“I got my hair colored a warm chocolate brown… and I’m loving it. The tones complement my jet-brown hair beautifully, with layers blending in naturally. Amy is an artist when it comes to color, balayage, and ombré.

She prefers working with natural tones and avoids bleach, which I appreciate because I’m all about natural products. The entire process took about four hours—color, wash, and style—and everything went smoothly. Amy was attentive, knowledgeable, and reassuring throughout.

I was offered a snack and water, watched TV, and relaxed while her team worked. Her husband and daughter were also around—friendly and helpful. I’m really pleased with the results. Thanks Amy and staff!”
