Minimal. Clean. Sleek.

That’s the immediate impression when stepping into **Luce Ave Coffee Roasters**, a centrally located café in Houston’s **Greenway/Inner Loop area**. This narrow yet thoughtfully designed coffee bar blends modern aesthetics with in-house roasting, creating a refined space for espresso lovers and casual coffee drinkers alike.

With its quiet ambiance and contemporary feel, Luce Ave makes for an ideal stop whether you’re meeting a friend, taking a mid-day break, or simply exploring Houston’s coffee scene.

## The Atmosphere

Upon entering, you’ll notice how intimate the space feels — narrow, streamlined, and thoughtfully arranged. A few tables line the wall, making the most of the compact footprint.

Toward the back of the café sits the coffee roasting machine, paired with colorful artwork that adds personality to the otherwise sleek setting. The blend of industrial elements and warm details gives the café a unique charm.

The overall vibe? Light, calm, and perfect for conversation.

## What We Ordered

### ☕ Peppermint Mocha Latte

In the spirit of the winter season, the peppermint mocha latte was the drink of choice. Visually, it carried a slightly murky green hue — but flavor-wise, it delivered.

The latte was strong yet smooth, balancing espresso depth with minty sweetness. It wasn’t overly sugary, allowing the coffee to remain the focal point.

### 🍪 Chocolate Chip Cookie

Paired with the latte was a chunky chocolate chip cookie — likely made in-house. It was generously studded with chocolate and deeply rich in flavor. The texture leaned on the firmer side, slightly overbaked, but still satisfying alongside a warm drink.

Other pastry offerings included muffins and additional baked goods.

## Service

The baristas were welcoming, knowledgeable, and attentive. They answered questions thoroughly and even brought water to the table after seating — a thoughtful touch that elevated the overall experience.

## On the Radar

One menu item that stands out: the **Earl Grey latte** — a unique tea-based drink not commonly found at most Houston cafés. As a tea enthusiast, this is definitely on the list for the next visit.

Luce Ave Coffee Roasters offers a polished coffee experience in Houston’s Inner Loop. From in-house roasting to seasonal lattes and attentive service, it’s a café that balances quality and comfort.

Whether you’re craving a smooth espresso drink, exploring unique tea lattes, or just looking for a minimalist space to unwind, Luce Ave is worth a visit.
