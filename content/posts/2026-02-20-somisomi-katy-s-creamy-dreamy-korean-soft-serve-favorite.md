SomiSomi in Katy delivers the kind of soft-serve experience that’s equal parts nostalgic, playful, and indulgently creamy. Though the interior is small—much like most of their locations—the grab-and-go setup works perfectly for this bustling dessert spot. Lines may form quickly, but they move fast, and the reward of warm taiyaki paired with cold, silky soft serve is always worth it.

This location shines with its rotating flavors, offering something new and exciting on every visit. From classic staples to unique seasonal blends, SomiSomi keeps dessert lovers curious and coming back. The Korean-style soft serve is velvety, milky, lightly sweet, and wonderfully airy—ideal for customizing with toppings, fillings, and that iconic fish-shaped waffle cone.

**Must-Try Items:**

- Korean-style soft serve (milky, fluffy & not overly sweet)
- Seasonal flavors — sweet corn + horchata
- Taiyaki waffle filled with vanilla custard
- Matcha-dusted soft serve
- Any swirl or mix for added creaminess

## **Review**

Sara I. (Nov 2, 2024): 

“SomiSomi is so so yummy yummy.

I'm liking this location out in Katy. A little small within the interior, but most locations seem to be that way. It's a grab and go ice cream spot that fills up quickly. The lines are long yet it moves rather fast. Customer service is good and you get to watch your dessert being made. I've always liked Korean style soft serve. So fluffy, soft and creamy. Very milky as well and not too sweet. They seem to often have a new flavor and that is what I like about this spot. They had a sweet corn flavor mixed with their horchata flavor and it was good. A bit more on the mellow side but it still has a nice aroma and flavor. Their crispy fish shaped waffle is so yummy. I love loading it up with vanilla custard. Their dusting of matcha on top of soft serve is chef's kiss. Flavors are good and portions are plenty.

You can't go wrong with SomiSomi as a cooling and creamy dessert option after having savory bite to eat within the area. With so much to see and do in this part of town, it's no wonder that SomiSomi is on the list for something delectable while out in this part of town. Creamy and dreamy. Until we meet again!”
