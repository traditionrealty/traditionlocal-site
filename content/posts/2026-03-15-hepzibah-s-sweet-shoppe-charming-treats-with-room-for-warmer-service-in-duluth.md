Hepzibah’s Sweet Shoppe sits tucked inside a cozy Duluth shopping area, offering a whimsical assortment of chocolates, classic candies, and local sweet creations. The display cases are filled with rich chocolate truffles, English toffee, and nostalgic treats—the kind that spark curiosity and childhood memories. But while the confections deliver on flavor, the service experience doesn’t quite match the charm of the shop’s sugary offerings.

During this visit, the service felt cold, distant, and unexpectedly curt—an ironic contrast in a candy boutique. With premium prices (nearly $20 for a small selection of treats), warmer hospitality would have made the experience more inviting. Still, the truffles themselves were undeniably decadent: smooth, rich, and full of deep chocolate flavor. The English toffee looked promising for a future taste test.

Hepzibah’s remains a sweet stop for those wandering Duluth’s boutiques, especially for travelers with a sweet tooth. With a little extra attention to friendliness, this shoppe could easily match the charm of its treats.

**Worth Trying:**

- Chocolate truffles (rich & flavorful)
- English toffee for a future visit
- Local and nostalgic candies

## **Review**

Sara I. (May 3, 2023):

“Service was just okay. Walked in yesterday and experienced cold and awkward service. The shoppe owner seemed a bit indifferent and crass as well. Totally ironic within a sweet candy shoppe.

The candy itself was neat — a few local products and many chocolate truffles to choose from. Plenty of generic candies as well that you'll find at other spots. Although the candy itself was good, it was way overpriced. Just a few pieces of candy cost close to $20.

The bottom line? Chocolate truffles were delicious. Decadent and full of rich chocolate flavor. The English toffee looked good as well. I may get that next time I revisit Duluth. Service needs a bit of a revamp though. It's a small little shoppe. You can't miss it if you're shopping and browsing within the area.

Try it for yourself if you have a sweet tooth. Here's hoping you have a sweeter experience.”
