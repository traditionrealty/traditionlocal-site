Kolache Shoppe in Greenway is the definition of a warm, comforting morning stop—tucked quietly into a cozy corner of Houston’s inner loop. This small but inviting bakery is perfect for those early hours when you need something fresh, quick, and reliably satisfying. With a closing time of **12 pm sharp**, it’s the kind of place you want to get to early, especially before your favorite kolaches sell out.

The menu offers a solid mix of classic meat-filled kolaches, vegetarian options, and beloved staples like egg-and-cheese, which is a hit even with little ones. Each kolache is tender, fluffy, and generously filled—simple, delicious fuel for busy mornings. Pair your pick with a cup of their hot coffee, and you’ve got the makings of a cozy, grab-and-go breakfast moment.

While the shop itself is small and leans more toward takeout, there are a few outdoor tables available for a quick sit-down. Service is consistently friendly, and the kolaches are always warm and fresh, making it a reliable neighborhood favorite. Dessert kolaches are on the menu too—perfect for a future visit if you’ve got a sweet tooth waiting to be indulged.

**Must-Try Items:**

- Egg & Cheese Kolache
- Brisket Kolache
- Vegetarian Kolache options
- Hot Coffee
- Dessert Kolaches (for next time!)

This is a nice spot to visit and venture off to your weekend hangouts or errands!”
