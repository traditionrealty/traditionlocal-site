Tucked into a charming Upper Kirby neighborhood, Path Of Tea is a lovely little tea shop that feels like a quiet retreat from the busy Houston pace. After a relaxed afternoon at Trader Joe’s, my twin sister and I finally stopped in to explore this spot we had been meaning to try.

As tea enthusiasts with a cabinet full of blends at home, we were excited to see such a thoughtful and diverse selection available here.

If you are searching for:

- Best tea shops in Houston
- Tea rooms in Upper Kirby
- Caffeine free tea options Houston
- Cozy places to drink tea in Houston

Path Of Tea is a beautiful option.

## Why You Will Love It

- Extensive tea selection
- Caffeine free options available
- Cozy and intimate atmosphere
- Choose your own tea mug experience
- Light desserts that pair well with tea

## The Atmosphere

The shop is small, warm, and inviting. A few guests were chatting with friends while others worked quietly on laptops. It has that peaceful tea room energy that encourages you to slow down and savor the moment.

One charming detail is that you get to choose the mug you sip your tea from. The display of mugs adds personality to the experience and makes it feel personal and intentional.

They also carry tea products and accessories available for purchase.

## What We Ordered

### Yummy Bear Tea

Since we wanted something caffeine free, we chose the Yummy Bear Tea.

It arrived fresh and piping hot. The flavor was fruity with subtle floral notes, soothing and aromatic without being overwhelming. A perfect blend for a relaxed afternoon.

### Mini Hazelnut and Chocolate Cake

The small cake paired beautifully with the tea. Lightly sweet and satisfying without overpowering the delicate flavors of the blend.

## Overall Impression

Path Of Tea offers a thoughtful tea experience in a cozy neighborhood setting. From the wide selection of blends to the charming mug display, everything feels intentional and welcoming.

As self proclaimed tea lovers, we left impressed and eager to return to try more varieties. It is the kind of place where you can easily spend an afternoon sipping and relaxing.
