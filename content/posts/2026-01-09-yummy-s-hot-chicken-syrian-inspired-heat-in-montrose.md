***[MOVED LOCATIONS - From Galleria to Montrose]***

Yummy’s Hot Chicken takes the ever-popular hot chicken trend and elevates it with a bold Syrian twist. This unassuming food truck delivers layers of flavor, crunch, and spice that set it apart from the typical fried-chicken lineup. The menu may be small, but each item is crafted with intention—crispy textures, vibrant toppings, and spice levels that range from gentle heat to full-on fire.

The chicken stays remarkably juicy beneath its golden crust, and the bright slaw adds a refreshing snap that balances the richness. Fans of creative comfort food will appreciate the unique touches, including Middle Eastern-inspired seasoning blends and their standout curry fries. Grab your order early, especially in hot weather—the food is worth savoring before the Houston heat kicks in.

### **Must-Try Items**

- **Syrian Hot Chicken Sandwich (medium spice recommended)** – crisp, fresh, full of layered flavor
- **Curry Fries** – fragrant curry sauce blended with cheese; hot, bold, and delicious
- **Chicken Tender Basket** – same addictive seasoning in tender form
- **Challah Bun Option** – soft, slightly sweet, and a perfect match for the heat

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Aug 16, 2020):** 

“Hot. Crispy. Chicken sandwich. But with a Syrian twist.

This sandwich was *hecka* good—lots of flavor, layers, and textures. Yummy’s has a small, simple menu, but every item overdelivers. I rarely eat anything fried, but their unique twist puts this sandwich in a league of its own. The slaw is bright and vibrant, and the chicken crust is crisp and golden.

They offer different spice levels; I chose medium and it was just right. The curry fries were surprisingly delicious—an interesting fusion with curry sauce meshed into mild cheese sauce, and they arrived steaming hot. My sandwich came on a challah bun instead of a pretzel bun, and I loved it. Definitely ask which bun they’re using.

Everything was crisp and yummy. This spot packs heat and taste. I’ll probably revisit in the fall—eating outside in summer was an adventure in itself! Thanks, Yummy’s. Hope y’all continue to thrive in the Galleria food truck park.”
