Tucked inside a busy Meyerland shopping plaza, Berripop Frozen Yogurt offers a lighter, tangier alternative to the overly sweet frozen yogurt chains around Houston. While many locations have sadly closed over the years, this Meyerland spot remains for those who prefer crisp, tart yogurt over candy loaded swirls.

If you are a fan of fresh fruit toppings and bright, pomegranate style flavors, Berripop may still be worth a visit.

## Why You Might Like It

- Tart yogurt options rather than overly sweet flavors
- Fresh fruit focused topping bar
- Smaller, quieter location
- Convenient Meyerland shopping plaza setting

## The Atmosphere

This Meyerland location is smaller than former Sugar Land and Rice Village spots. Seating is tighter, with booths that offer limited leg room.

It is a casual stop while wandering through the surrounding stores and outlets. The plaza itself is lively and convenient, making it easy to pop in for a quick dessert break.

## What I Ordered

### Pomegranate Yogurt with Fresh Fruit

I have always loved Berripop for its crisp and tart yogurt flavors. I prefer that bright tang over the overly sweet style found at other chains.

The yogurt itself was still enjoyable, even on a rather cold day. However, the fruit bar was disappointing during this visit. The “fresh fruit” looked unappealing and somewhat dried out.

Fortunately, the employee behind the counter had a fresh container of strawberries and brought them out after I mentioned it. Had I not said anything, I would have likely received fruit from the older batch.

The yogurt remained crisp and satisfying, but freshness at the topping bar is clearly something this location needs to maintain more carefully.

## Overall Impression

Berripop has always been a favorite for its tart and refreshing yogurt flavors. While this Meyerland location keeps the brand alive, the quality of the topping bar may vary.

If they improve the freshness of the fruit, it would elevate the experience significantly. For now, it is a decent stop when craving tangy frozen yogurt, but perhaps not a destination worthy of a special trip.
