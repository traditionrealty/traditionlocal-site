## The Food

Brunch tables filled quickly with comforting classics:

- 🌮 Breakfast tacos
- 🍳 Eggs and traditional plates
- 🍲 Chilaquiles
- ☕ Hot locally sourced coffee (from Katz)

The flavors were warm, fresh, and satisfying — exactly what you want on a rainy morning. The chilaquiles delivered that perfect balance of saucy and savory, while the tacos kept things simple and authentic.

And the coffee? Smooth, comforting, and locally sourced through Katz — always a reliable choice to round out a leisurely brunch.

## The Atmosphere

Rainy weather tends to slow things down in the best way. The cozy setting allowed everyone to settle in, linger over coffee, and enjoy conversation without feeling rushed.

We stayed for a few hours, soaking in the relaxed energy and friendly service. It’s the kind of brunch that feels less like a meal and more like a mini weekend retreat.
