## What We Tried

### 🍕 Kebab Pizza

Loaded with generous amounts of meat, this pie leans savory and hearty. The flavors are bold and filling — ideal for those who enjoy substantial toppings.

### 🍕 Chicken Tikka Pizza

A fusion favorite.

The chicken tikka topping brings warmth and spice, creating a pizza that feels more South Asian-inspired than traditional Italian. It’s flavorful, satisfying, and definitely not your standard slice.

## Crust & Cheese Notes

A few observations:

- The crust was thicker and slightly dry for my personal preference.
- Some pizzas incorporate cheddar cheese, which may surprise those accustomed to mozzarella-based pies.

If you’re used to European-style pizzas with lighter crusts and white cheeses like mozzarella, ricotta, or fontina, this will feel different. Pizza 101 leans more toward bold, American-style builds.

## Portion & Flavor

- Generous toppings
- Slightly on the salty side
- Very filling

These pizzas are substantial — great for feeding a group or satisfying a serious appetite.

## Overall Impression

Pizza 101 stands out in Sugar Land for its specialty, fusion-inspired pizzas. While the crust and cheese style may not appeal to everyone, the bold toppings and hearty portions make it a solid option for adventurous eaters or large gatherings.

If you’re curious about chicken tikka or kebab on pizza — this is the place to try it.
