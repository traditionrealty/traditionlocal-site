Nails by Yen has evolved over the years into a reliably warm, clean, and friendly neighborhood salon. Known for its large massage-chair pedicure stations, vivid polish selection, and consistently tidy setup, it remains a local favorite for simple manicures, relaxing pedicures, and quick self-care appointments. Technicians are attentive, personable, and often remember returning clients—building a sense of familiarity that keeps many regulars coming back.

### **What to Expect**

- Comfortable massage-chair pedicure stations
- Friendly technicians who remember regular clients
- Clean, well-maintained salon environment
- Good polish selection with bright, fresh colors
- Appointment reminders and courtesy calls
- Discounted basic manicures on select weekdays

### **Reviews**

**Sara I. (Dec 15, 2021):**

 “Great service—it has improved over the years. Many of the nail technicians are friendly and will remember you if you visit often. Joanne and Audrey are always friendly and socialize with everyone. This spot has remained nice and clean over time, with plenty of comfortable pedicure seats. They’ll offer drinks if you ask; I typically go for a water bottle. They also have a vivid selection of nail polish that’s never stale. Convenient, courteous, and prompt—they’ll even call if you're running late. This place has been a family favorite for years. Until next time!”

**Sara I. (Jul 21, 2016):** 

“I came here about once a month and was made to feel like a stranger. Some customers received warm greetings while others didn’t, which was disappointing. I was never asked if I wanted water until I finally asked for it on a hot day. Nonetheless, the manicures and pedicures were consistent, especially on Mondays and Tuesdays when the basic manicure is discounted. I appreciated the clean polish look and occasional decorative nail art.”

**Sara I. (May 15, 2016):**

 “Great service. I often came on Mondays or Tuesdays for their $10 basic manicure—it always got the job done. If you paid in cash, you received a small discount. Clean, reliable, and relaxing. The deluxe pedicure was lovely and pampering.”
