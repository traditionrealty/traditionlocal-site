Nestled quietly among the trees of Two Harbors, **Cedar Coffee Company** is a charming, woodsy escape for travelers venturing along Minnesota’s North Shore. Warm, inviting, and tucked just enough off the main path to feel like a discovery, this café blends handcrafted drinks, nourishing food, and serene outdoor seating into a memorable stop for hikers, vacationers, and coffee lovers alike.

The menu shines with house-made touches—from syrups to pastries to savory items. Their matcha latte, sweetened with cardamom syrup, is an unexpected dream pairing: floral, earthy, warm, and beautifully balanced. Pastries like the blueberry coconut scone are tender, buttery, and freshly baked, perfect alongside morning drinks. For heartier appetites, Cedar Coffee offers flavorful sandwiches and wraps, including a standout Thai-style vegetarian wrap filled with curried veggies, peanut sauce, and toasted lavash.

Surrounded by towering trees and gentle breezes, the outdoor seating area feels like a peaceful retreat, making Cedar Coffee Company an ideal pre-hike or mid-adventure recharge spot.

**Must-Try Items:**

- Matcha latte with house-made cardamom syrup
- Blueberry coconut scone
- Thai-style vegetarian wrap with curried veggies & peanut sauce
- Chicken sandwich for a heartier meal
- Any of their house-made syrups or baked goods

## **Review**

Sara I. (Jul 7, 2023):

“This place was awesome! All huddled and cuddled within a quiet corner of Two Harbors. I love that city and it was pretty neat finding this coffee shoppe on a whim whilst traveling through the north shores.

Coffee was great as well as their matcha latte. I've been digging warm matcha latte and asked for it to be sweetened with cardamom syrup. Absolutely phenomenal. Cardamom pairs sooo well with matcha — what a matcha made in sight! No pun intended.

Everything is practically made in house here, including their house made syrups, baked goods and savory food items. I loved their in house made scone... warm, buttery and tender. They had a few scone selections to choose from, but we went for the blueberry coconut scone. Tasty and paired well with our beverages.

We were also hungry and grabbed their chicken sandwich as well as their Thai style wrap. The wrap consisted of curried veggies, spicy Thai style peanut sauce wrapped up in a toasted lavash wrap bread. Yum. The roasted veggies had an Indian style flavor and paired well with the peanut sauce tucked in — very fusion style wrap. It was nice to eat a flavorful vegetarian lunch item that day, whilst traveling through the city.

Indoors seating was plentiful but it was nice to sit outside in their backyard, immersed within a grove of many trees. Weather was refreshing in regards to MN style summers and a perfect little spot to visit before venturing out to a hike.

I recommend Cedar Coffee if in the Two Harbors area. It's lively and adds a splash of coffee color before journeying towards your next adventure. Until next time!”
