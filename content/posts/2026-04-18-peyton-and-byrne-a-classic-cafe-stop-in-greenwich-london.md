Just steps away from the charming **Greenwich Market in London**, Peyton and Byrne is the kind of bakery café that effortlessly draws you in. Between the elegant pastry displays and the inviting, classic British café aesthetic, it’s hard not to pause and step inside.

Whether you’re exploring the historic streets of Greenwich, browsing market stalls, or gallery-hopping in the neighborhood, this patisserie makes for a lovely lunch or tea stop.

If you’re searching for:

- Best café near Greenwich Market
- Lunch in Greenwich London
- British bakery Greenwich
- Tea and pastries Greenwich

Peyton and Byrne is well worth the visit.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Prime location near Greenwich Market
- Beautiful pastry and cake displays
- Fresh salads, tarts, and sandwiches
- Elegant tea selection
- Classic London café charm

## The Atmosphere

On a Saturday afternoon, the café was bustling — a clear sign of its popularity. Despite the crowd, the space felt warm and inviting, with polished counters lined with cakes, pastries, and savory tarts.

The overall vibe leans classic and refined, making it a standout among the many café options in the area.

## What to Order

### 🥗 Grain & Herb Salad

Crisp, fresh, and brimming with fragrant herbs. The grains were well-balanced and light, making it both satisfying and wholesome.

### 🥧 Honey Baked Gooseberry & Stilton Tart

A beautiful vegetarian pairing. The tart featured:

- Buttery, flaky crust
- Creamy stilton cheese
- Sweet, mellow gooseberries

The sweet-savory contrast worked wonderfully and complemented the freshness of the salad.

### 🌹 Rose Tea (with Honey)

Served in a pot, the rose tea was soothing and aromatic. A touch of honey elevated the delicate floral notes and paired beautifully with the meal.

## Coffee & Pastries

Beyond savory options, Peyton and Byrne offers:

- Fresh sandwiches
- Seasonal salads
- Savory tarts
- Cakes and pastries
- Quality coffee

Everything appears freshly prepared and thoughtfully displayed — perfect for a quick yet refined meal.

## Service

Service felt slightly rushed due to the busy weekend crowd, but that’s understandable for a popular café in a high-traffic area.

## Overall Impression

Peyton and Byrne delivers a polished yet approachable café experience in one of London’s most charming neighborhoods. From fresh vegetarian options to beautifully presented pastries and soothing tea, it’s an ideal stop while exploring Greenwich.

Among the diverse café scene in the area, this one stands out for its classic appeal and well-executed menu.
