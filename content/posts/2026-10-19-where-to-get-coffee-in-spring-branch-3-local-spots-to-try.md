Some mornings call for coffee and a pastry. Others deserve a full breakfast and time to catch up with a friend. In Houston’s Spring Branch neighborhood, *Cambrian Coffee, Buzz & Bites, and Slowpokes* offer three ways to make a coffee run a little more interesting. All with reported Google ratings above four stars.

## Cambrian Coffee: Coffee and British Baking

*9461 Hammerly Blvd., Suite E · 4.8★*

The pairing to start with at Cambrian is simple: a latte flavored with honey and cinnamon and a British sausage roll. The café makes its syrups in house and bakes everything from scones with cheese and chives to cookies and seasonal mini loaves.

If you usually drink your coffee black, the menu also offers pour over coffee, cold brew, and nitro cold brew. Come hungry, and check the rotating sausage roll special before deciding. The classic is served daily until it sells out.

[Website](https://cambriancoffeehtx.com/) · [Menu](https://cambriancoffeehtx.com/menu) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Cambrian+Coffee+9461+Hammerly+Blvd+Houston+TX)

## Buzz & Bites: When Coffee Turns Into Brunch

*1444 Blalock Rd., Suite G · 4.6★*

For the friend who always asks, “Have you eaten yet?” there’s Buzz & Bites. This neighborhood coffee shop and eatery lists brunch served all day, with options including an croissant with avocado and bacon, breakfast wraps, and French toast topped with strawberries and blueberries.

There’s plenty to choose from when breakfast rolls into lunch, too, including paninis, salads, and avocado toast. Make this your pick when you want coffee and a proper meal at the same table.

[Website](https://buzznbites.com/) · [Menu](https://buzznbites.com/food-menu) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Buzz+and+Bites+1444+Blalock+Rd+Houston+TX)

## Slowpokes: A Coffee Stop on Long Point

*8147B Long Point Rd. · 4.6★*

Slowpokes’ Spring Branch location makes an easy choice when everyone wants something different. The menu ranges from cappuccinos and cold brew to breakfast bagels, sandwiches, and flatbreads.

Start with a latte and breakfast bagel for a morning visit, or try the chicken pesto panino at lunch. If you find a coffee you’d like to brew at home, Slowpokes also sells whole beans roasted in Houston.

[Website](https://www.imaslowpoke.com/) · [Location & Menu](https://order.imaslowpoke.com/order/?branchId=650af11d257f685b65babcff&branchName=Spring+Branch) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Slowpokes+8147B+Long+Point+Rd+Houston+TX)

*Which should you try first?* Choose Cambrian for British baked goods, Buzz & Bites for brunch served all day, or Slowpokes for coffee and lunch on Long Point.

*Google rating references: Cambrian Coffee, Buzz & Bites, and Slowpokes. Ratings may change.*
