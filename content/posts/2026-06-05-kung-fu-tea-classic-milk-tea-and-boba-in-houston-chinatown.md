Located in the heart of Houston Chinatown, Kung Fu Tea is a popular destination for crisp, refreshing bubble tea and milk tea drinks. On a busy Saturday night, it still managed to deliver a smooth and efficient experience, which says a lot for a well loved boba spot.

Whether you are meeting friends or satisfying a sweet craving, this café offers a lively yet comfortable environment to enjoy Taiwanese style beverages.

If you are searching for:

- Best bubble tea in Houston Chinatown
- Milk tea with tapioca Houston
- Boba shops in Chinatown Houston
- Jasmine milk tea Houston
- Late night bubble tea Houston

Kung Fu Tea is a solid choice.

## Why You Will Like It

- Quick and efficient service, even on busy nights
- Perfectly cooked tapioca pearls
- Light and frothy milk tea texture
- Adjustable sweetness and ice levels
- Spacious seating for groups

## The Atmosphere

Kung Fu Tea has plenty of tables throughout the café, making it a great spot to gather with friends. There are small tables for intimate chats and larger ones for groups.

It can get noisy on the weekends due to the crowd, but the energy feels lively rather than overwhelming. Even close to closing time, the staff did not rush us out, which was appreciated.

## What I Ordered

### Iced Milk Tea with Tapioca

My go to drink at almost any tapioca shop is iced milk tea with tapioca. In a hot city like Houston, iced always wins.

The drink was made quickly, and everyone in my group received their beverages at the same time. The milk tea was crisp, creamy, and lightly frothy. There was a subtle floral jasmine scent that added a delicate touch.

The tapioca pearls were perfect. Firm, chewy, and lightly sweet. The ice to milk ratio was balanced, and the sweetness level was just right without needing any adjustments.

The machines they use seem to create a light and airy texture, which makes the drink especially refreshing.

Prices may feel slightly steep, but for an occasional treat, it satisfies the craving.

## Overall Impression

Kung Fu Tea delivers a consistent and enjoyable milk tea experience in Houston Chinatown. From the quick service to the perfectly chewy tapioca pearls, it hits the mark for a classic boba run.

If you are craving a crisp and creamy milk tea with tapioca, this is a dependable spot to visit.
