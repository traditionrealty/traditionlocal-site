Mazajj Organic Coffee brings a refreshing global warmth to the Galleria/Uptown area, offering an inviting space filled with natural light, stylish décor, and some of the city’s most lovingly crafted chai. With a freshly opened storefront closer to Houston’s inner loop than their Katy location, this café has already become a neighborhood favorite for anyone seeking a cozy, cultural, and aromatic coffeehouse experience.

The atmosphere blends modern elegance with international charm—sunlight pouring through large windows, warm hospitality from the staff, and ambient music that feels transportive. Whether you’re settling in for a quiet morning, an evening wind-down, or a meetup with friends, Mazajj’s ambiance is as memorable as its drinks.

**Must-Try Items:**

- **Adeni Chai** (light, creamy & perfectly balanced; available by the cup or teapot)
- Coffee selections made with quality beans
- Desserts including viral Dubai-style chocolate & milk cakes
- Complimentary treats during special events or openings

## **Reviews**

Sara I. (Sep 17, 2024 – First to Review, ROTD Dec 22, 2024): 

“Absolutely wonderful interior, lots of indoor lighting as well as natural lighting pouring in through the windows. Great customer service and warm hospitality, thanks guys!

This Mazajj is a lot closer within the inner loop of Houston compared to their Katy location. Their Katy shoppe is just as lovely though, with similar interior deco and piping hot tea.

Choices for teas and beverages are plenty. Want coffee? They've got it. Want tea? They have different styles. My favorite being the Adeni chai. It's not too strong as other spots make it. A bit of a lighter and creamier flavor. I always opt for half sweet and it's perfect. You can get it by the cup or as a teapot. I love their teapot and glassware. Adorable and makes for a cozy morning or evening spent sipping on chai. Warm and exotic music playing in the background makes for such an international vibe. I'm loving how they're situated within the Galleria area. We really needed a spot for authentic chai and Mazajj delivers! They just had their grand opening 2 days ago & already got it down in terms of customer service. Thanks for the complimentary desserts today. So sweet.

Y'all, check out Mazajj and thank me later. Support local and support chai that's made from scratch with true warmth and passion. Try their desserts, viral Dubai chocolate and milk cakes. So good and so worth it. Welcome to the neighborhood!”
