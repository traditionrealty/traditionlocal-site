La La Land Kind Café brings all the dreamy, feel-good charm—white picket fence, golden sunlight, cozy porch seating, and a whimsical interior that looks straight out of a movie set. It’s bright, uplifting, and known just as much for its kindness-driven mission as it is for its standout drinks and treats. Warm service and a soft, airy vibe make this an ideal stop whether you're catching up with a friend or indulging in a solo “main character” moment.

### **⭐ What to Order**

- **House-Made Chocolate Chip Cookie** – Gigantic, Levain-style, craggy, gooey, and absolutely worth the hype.
- **Butterfly Matcha Latte** – Insta-pretty, layered, and floral with a gentle sweetness.
- **Lavender Bloom Latte** – Soft, fragrant, and relaxing without being overly perfumey.
- **Iced Matcha or Iced Chai** – Smooth, creamy, and refreshing for warm Dallas days.

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Nov 25, 2019):**

 “I feel like I’ve stepped into a movie set walking into this place—white picket fence, cozy porch, and a polished, ultra-preppy interior. Not quite the hipster coffee scene of Houston, but charming in its own sunshine-filled way.

Definitely try the in-house chocolate chip cookie. It’s mega huge with crag-like surfaces—basically the Levain cookie of Texas. And y’all, I bake some of the best c.c. cookies, and this one was good.

I prefer exotic flavors, so the cardamom latte was my pick. Paired beautifully with that cookie. Bring a friend, take some cute photos—or don’t. Just enjoy the warm, kind atmosphere of La La Land Kind Cafe. Truly.”
