***[PERMANENTLY CLOSED - Store No Longer Operating]*** 

Lamppost Coffee brings soft minimalism, clean design, and a warm welcome to the Austin café scene. The shop’s airy simplicity pairs perfectly with its creative drink menu, offering both classic espresso staples and unique specialty lattes you won’t find in every coffeehouse. Friendly baristas and a flexible approach to customization make it an easy stop for anyone who knows exactly how they like their drink crafted.

With a solid mix of coffee, tea, pastries, and small bites, it’s a lovely place for an afternoon recharge or a quiet moment alone.

### **What to Try**

- **Iced Matcha Latte** – smooth, creamy, customizable, not overly sweet
- **Signature Lattes** – inventive flavor combos unique to Lamppost
- **Pastries & light bites** – fresh options to pair with your bevvie

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jun 22, 2021):** 

“I adore the minimal, clean, yet simple aesthetic of this coffee shoppe! The name is just as charming. The folks running the shop are friendly and can whip up any beverage to your liking. They have some unique lattes on their menu—flavors I haven’t seen elsewhere.

I ordered an iced matcha latte with half whole milk and half skim, plus half vanilla syrup. They customized it perfectly. The drink was smooth, not too sweet, and such a refreshing pick-me-up for the afternoon. I’ll definitely revisit and try more drinks off their menu.”
