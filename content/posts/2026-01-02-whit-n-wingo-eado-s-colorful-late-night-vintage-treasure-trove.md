Whit N Wingo is the kind of vintage shop that immediately pulls you in with its color, charm, and treasure-hunt energy. Nestled in EaDo along Harrisburg, this vibrant boutique is packed wall-to-wall with personality—rings, tote bags, clothing, knick-knacks, playful trinkets, and an ever-changing mix of curiosities. It’s a space made for wandering, exploring, and discovering something unexpected.

One of the standout features? **They stay open late**—a rare treat in the world of vintage retail. Whether you're grabbing ice cream, finishing up pizza in the plaza, or simply looking for a fun nighttime stroll, Whit N Wingo offers a warm, whimsical place to browse. Friendly service (shoutout to Zach!), welcoming vibes, and a rainbow of items make it an unforgettable stop for fashion lovers and curious shoppers alike.

**Why You’ll Love It:**

- Late-night browsing for night owls and explorers
- A colorful selection of vintage jewelry, clothing, and quirky finds
- Friendly, knowledgeable staff
- Located next to other fun shops, perfect for post-dinner exploring
- Kid-friendly trinkets and tote bags for gifting

## **Reviews**

Sara I. (Jun 8, 2025 – First to Review):

 “Quite a unique vintage shoppe, very colorful with so many items on display and for purchase. I found a few cute rings here and I just love wearing them. I met with Zach and he was friendly and knowledgeable about the shoppe. He explained to me that they stay opened late, so this is definitely a spot you can hang out late at night and just browse around. They have plenty of knick-knacks and cool stuff overall. Whit N Wingo is right by a few other fun stores, so definitely drop by here after getting some ice cream or pizza in the same plaza. They had some nice tote bags here a few little toys that I would get for my son next time. Overall, a very colorful shoppe to stop by when in the Harrisburg (Eado) area.”
