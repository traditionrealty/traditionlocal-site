## *A Mediterranean Grille for Shared Plates, Grilled Favorites, and Relaxed Houston Dining*

**Butcher’s Grille** is a Houston Mediterranean restaurant located at **5740 San Felipe St, 100**. It is a fitting choice for diners looking for grilled meats, savory dips, warm pita, generous portions, and a casual atmosphere that works well for both small and large groups.

For anyone searching for a **Mediterranean restaurant in Houston with outdoor seating, vegan options, and a kids’ menu**, Butcher’s Grille offers a flexible dining experience for friends, families, and mixed dining groups.

## What Makes Butcher’s Grille Stand Out

Butcher’s Grille is built around bold Mediterranean flavors and table-friendly dishes. The menu leans into grilled items, shawarma-style meals, Levantine dips, warm pita, hummus, and savory plates that are easy to enjoy with others.

It is especially useful for group dining because the food is satisfying, shareable, and portioned generously. Whether guests are planning a casual Friday night dinner, a family meal, or a relaxed night out with friends, the restaurant offers a comfortable setting for gathering around the table.

## Dining Features and Service Options

- **Outdoor seating**
- **Vegan options**
- **Kids’ menu**

These features make Butcher’s Grille a practical option for different dining needs. Outdoor seating gives guests another way to enjoy the space, vegan options help accommodate plant-forward diners, and the kids’ menu makes the restaurant more family-friendly.

## What to Order for a First Visit

For a first visit, start with a dip for the table, add warm pita, and choose a grilled or shawarma-style entrée. This gives diners a strong introduction to the restaurant’s strengths: bold seasonings, creamy dips, hearty plates, and shared Mediterranean comfort food.

## Menu Highlights

- **Roasted Red Pepper and Walnut Muhammara Dip:** A Levantine-style appetizer with rich, savory flavor. Best paired with warm pita and shared at the start of the meal.
- **Chicken Shawarma Meal:** A hearty Mediterranean plate with bold seasoning and satisfying flavor.
- **Creamy Hummus:** A smooth, classic side that pairs well with pita, shawarma, grilled items, and shared appetizers.

Come hungry, especially if you plan to order both appetizers and entrées. The portions are generous, and the menu works well for sharing. Groups may want to order several dips and mains for the table so everyone can try a variety of flavors.
