## What We Ordered

### 🥪 Wicked Philly Cheesesteak

The “Wicked Philly” was marketed as a spicy, meat-loaded sandwich featuring hot chili oil peppers — bold in description and promising in theory.

In execution, however, the sandwich fell short of expectations. The portion of meat was noticeably light, especially for a cheesesteak at its price point. The advertised chili oil peppers were not apparent, and caramelized onions were minimal at best.

For a sandwich described as hearty and spice-forward, it lacked the fullness and flavor depth anticipated.

## Overall Impression

Pappa Geno’s benefits from a convenient Montrose location and a clean, inviting interior. However, based on this visit, the execution of the featured cheesesteak did not align with its description — particularly in portion size and promised ingredients.

With strong competition in Houston’s late-night dining scene, especially in Montrose, consistency and value are key. For those seeking a fully loaded, generously portioned Philly cheesesteak, it may be worth exploring alternative options nearby.
