Taizzi Sushi brings a warm, unpretentious charm to the Katy dining scene. Located in a small, busy plaza near the mall, this compact restaurant offers an unexpectedly varied menu—from sushi to Korean ramen, bibimbap, and poke bowls. Guests can choose between à la carte dining or their popular AYCE option, making it a flexible pick for anything from a quick meal to a leisurely sushi night.

Despite its tight interior, Taizzi delivers impressively attentive service, fast pacing, and a relaxed, dimly lit atmosphere that suits casual dinners. If you enjoy baked sushi, spice-forward rolls, or comforting fusion dishes, this is a welcoming spot to roll with the tides.

### **Must-Try Items:**

- **Baked Salmon Sushi** – tender, warm, and richly flavored
- **Baked Lobster Sushi** – next on the list for baked-sushi lovers
- **Peppered Tuna Sashimi** – bold with a satisfying kick
- **Pink Lady Roll** – flavorful, balanced, and eye-catching
- **Korean Ramen & Bibimbap** – hearty options beyond sushi

### **Reviews**

**Sara I. (Apr 17, 2022):** 

“Roll with the tides! Taizzi Sushi in Katy isn't too far from the local mall. Decided to stop by on a whim with a good friend for dinner. I thought the food here was pretty decent. They've got quite a large selection of grub to choose from the menu. I spotted Korean style ramen, bibimbap, and even poke bowls on the menu. This spot is known for their AYCE selection or just wing it a la carte.

Settled in for sushi because that's what this place is known for. First of all, the plaza is tiny and the restaurant is cramped as well. Surprisingly though on a Saturday night, we found decent parking and it wasn't too busy inside—most likely due to the holidays.

Service was impeccable. Since it’s a small space, the servers continuously checked up on us. One of them even offered me a fork since I'm not the biggest fan of using chopsticks. Lol.

My favorite item was the baked sushi. I've always been a fan of baked sushi and they've got a decent selection. The baked salmon sushi we ordered was tender and flavorful. Definitely want to try the baked lobster option next time. Runner up was the peppered tuna sashimi and the Pink Lady sushi—both had a bite and a kick to them.

The jasmine tea wasn't that good though; flat and lukewarm. The interior is dark and cozy, good for a dinner vibe. They've got a TV inside blasting Korean music to keep you awake with all the glutinous rice intake. Wouldn’t mind revisiting when around the area—especially after shopping and settling in for cozy starch bombs. That's what sushi is after all, am I right? Lol. Keep it local & prosper! Until next time. :)”
