A laid-back evening stroll through Katy Asian Town set the tone for a cozy crawl filled with good conversation, flavorful bites, and plenty of laughs. Cool weather, warm company, and a lineup of diverse eateries made this gathering the perfect way to unwind at the end of the week. From street-style fusion snacks to whimsical desserts, the crawl highlighted the rich, evolving food culture of this bustling district.

### **Event Highlights:**

- **YELO Café** – Malaysian/Chinese/Vietnamese fusion bites
- **Shared plates & iced teas** – casual, communal nibbling
- **White Rabbit cake stop** – nostalgic, fun, and creamy
- **Multiple café visits** – conversation-friendly, cozy interiors
- **Chill Friday night ambiance** – relaxed crowd and perfect weather

### **Reviews**

**Sara I. (Apr 3, 2022):** “Thanks to Mister Lou for creating this cozy crawl hangout. It was so nice to get out and about on a chill and calm Friday night. The weather was chill and so were the peeps. Just the way I like it.

We all noshed on some tasty and unique grub at YELO café. It's a street-style Asian eatery consisting of fusion Malaysian/Chinese/Vietnamese foods. We shared bites and sipped on refreshing iced tea. Lou has some awesome friends that I was introduced to; we all made our way toward a few cafes that night.

Relished a few jokes, ate some white rabbit cake, and hopped to the next spot. I love friendly and talkative people—this was a fun gathering and it warmed my heart. Thanks Lou, for hosting this event. I look forward to collaborating and I know many more awesome events are in the works. Until next time!”
