Lava Coffee delivers exactly what you want from a neighborhood café: warmth, personality, and thoughtfully crafted drinks. With its industrial–European blend of décor, glowing ambient bulbs, and plush seating, this Katy hideaway feels both stylish and comforting. It’s an ideal mid-afternoon stop after wandering through Katy Mills or exploring Katy Asian Town, offering signature lattes and a focused menu of pastries to pair with them.

The standout here is the hospitality—Sasha, the owner, brings genuine care and enthusiasm to each drink, making the space feel welcoming and personal. Locally sourced beans, balanced flavors, and a calm environment make Lava Coffee a spot worth seeking out whenever you’re in the area.

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jan 1, 2020):** 

“I’m loving the warm industrial-meets-European décor here—soft lights, cozy chairs, and a posh vibe tucked quietly within Katy Asia Town. Stopped in after shopping at Katy Mills, and it ended up being the perfect afternoon pick-me-up.

Sasha, the owner, was incredibly friendly and made me the Honey Bear Latte (honey, vanilla, cinnamon). I ordered it iced but received it hot instead; Sasha offered to remake it, but honestly, I’m glad the mix-up happened. The hot version was creamy, soothing, and wonderful. I can imagine the iced one would be just as good. Their signature drinks look great, and the tea selection is extensive.

The beans are locally sourced, aromatic, and comforting, and although the pastry case is small, the drinks are clearly the star. If this café were closer, I’d be here much more often. Worth the trip whenever you’re in the area. Support local whenever you can!”
