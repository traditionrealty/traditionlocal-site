Amorino brings a touch of European indulgence to Highland Village, offering artisanal gelato and sorbet crafted with premium ingredients—many imported from around the world. The shop is warm, polished, and often buzzing with people, especially on festive evenings when the surrounding plaza glows with lights and activity. Known for their signature gelato “flower” cones, vibrant sorbets, rich chocolate flavors, and elegant pastries, Amorino is a favorite local stop for anyone craving a refined dessert experience.

#### **What to Order**

- **Three-Flavor Sorbet Cup** – Passionfruit, Black Currant & Raspberry (bright, tart, and intensely fruity).
- **Chocolate Gelato** – Deep, decadent, and perfect for dairy lovers.
- **Macarons Filled with Gelato** – Petite, elegant, and ideal for a sweet add-on.

#### **Review**

**Sara I. (Dec 31, 2018):**

“Ahh! I'm a big fan of sorbet… and this place totally satisfied my craving for all things sorbet related. Amorino serves authentic gelato, waffles, chocolates, coffee beverages, and even pound cakes and macarons. The shop was packed the night I visited—Christmas night—and luckily Amorino was open in this lively part of town. The River Oaks District plaza was glowing with lights and people wandering around.

Amorino has quite a selection of sorbets and gelato, with many of the fruit elements imported from various countries. I chose the smallest cup, which fits three flavors. Without hesitation, I picked the passionfruit, black currant, and raspberry—side by side. I’m so glad I chose them. They were crisp, tart, and full of fresh fruit flavor. Honestly, some of the best sorbet I've ever had!

I definitely want to revisit this cute little shoppe to try their dairy-based gelato—several chocolate flavors caught my eye. Now I understand why Amorino is popular worldwide. It’s a bit on the pricier side, but absolutely worth trying.”
