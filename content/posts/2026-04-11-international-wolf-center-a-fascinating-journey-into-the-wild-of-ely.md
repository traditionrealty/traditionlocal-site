The **International Wolf Center** in Ely, MN offers an immersive, educational, and unforgettable glimpse into the world of wolves. Surrounded by the natural beauty of Minnesota’s Northwoods, this center blends museum-style learning with real-time wildlife observation, making it a standout stop for families, travelers, and nature enthusiasts alike.

From the moment visitors enter, the experience feels thoughtful and informative. Staff members introduce the resident wolf pack, share regional history, and present artifacts that help deepen understanding of wolf behavior, ecology, and conservation. A viewing area with large glass panels lets guests watch the wolves roam, interact, and carry out their daily routines in a sanctuary that mirrors their natural habitat—a rare and intimate window into their world.

The center also features a museum exhibit hall, hands-on learning materials, and a small theater showing a well-made video about wolf lifestyles, reintroduction efforts, and their role in Minnesota’s ecosystem. A curated gift shop adds a fun finishing touch for souvenirs and educational items.

Whether you're touring northern Minnesota or planning a family nature trip, the International Wolf Center is an enriching, memorable stop that brings visitors closer to the wild than they may have ever been before.

**Why You’ll Love It:**

- Up-close viewing of wolves in a natural-like sanctuary
- Knowledgeable staff with engaging presentations
- Artifacts, exhibits & interactive educational displays
- Film screening about wolf ecology and history
- Great for families, wildlife lovers & travelers exploring Ely
- Gift shop for wolf-themed books, toys & memorabilia

## **Review**

Sara I. (Jul 7, 2023):

“Such a cool little spot to view wolves in their natural habitat! Service was great and informative upon entering the wolf center. Staff was helpful in regards to the history of the wolves within the area and the pack of wolves that currently live at the center. It was a display they held and staff showed a few artifacts that were quite helpful in understanding wolves and their current living arrangements.

Everyone got to view the current wolves behind a glass wall and saw them in their natural wild sanctuary. It was pretty neat seeing how they interact in their daily life and how they are common to spot in the city of Ely.

They even have a museum section with further information and artifacts to learn from, as well as a small cinema to view a video. The video pertains to the lifestyle and the habits of the wolves and gives a general brief history of the wolves being introduced to the area.

I love how interactive this spot was, they had plenty of things to see and do. They also have a small gift shop to purchase items from. I can definitely see myself visiting this spot if in the area. Just seeing all the wolves within the city of Ely was neat. It's a great place to visit when venturing through the area up north MN. International Wolf Center is a must if bringing friends or family up north. Into the wild you go!”
