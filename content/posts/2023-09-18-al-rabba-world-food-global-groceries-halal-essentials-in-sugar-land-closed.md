***[PERMANENTLY CLOSED - Store No Longer Operating]*** 

Al Rabba World Food brings a wide-ranging, multicultural grocery experience to a quiet corner of Sugar Land. Housed in what was once a neighborhood Randall’s, the store spans a large footprint with sections that alternate between spacious minimalism and fully stocked aisles. The result is an unexpected little adventure—one where shelves of spices, teas, cookware, and imported goods meet a modern halal butcher and a broad produce selection.

Fresh herbs, vegetables, and fruit are easy to find here, and the halal meat section is well-organized, neatly packaged, and properly dated. Though tucked deep within New Territory, far from major roads, the market offers a welcome blend of South Asian ingredients, American staples, and a few charming kitchen items that make wandering the aisles surprisingly fun.

### **Must-Find Items**

- Fresh herbs, peppers, ginger
- Halal meats packaged and dated securely
- International pantry items
- Culinary equipment & dainty teacups
- Large bakery section (for future exploring!)

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jun 20, 2020):** 

“I’m not sure where the low ratings are coming from—I had a nice experience today. Yes, some parts of the store feel barren while others are fully packed, but honestly that made it a little adventurous to sift through everything.

They have a large bakery (which I didn’t explore) and a great selection of produce. The herbs, peppers, ginger—all fresh. I make ginger tea often, so finding good quality ginger was essential. The halal meat section was extensive as well, with everything packaged and dated properly.

This is a huge market, previously a Randall’s, so you still see touches of the old grocery layout alongside international items. I even spotted culinary equipment and delicate teacups that caught my eye.

Though it’s nestled deep inside New Territory and a bit out of the way, it’s refreshing to have a diverse, clean market in Sugar Land. I won’t frequent it often due to distance, but I’m glad it’s here.”
