Looking for a bakery in Houston’s Greater Heights? Angela’s Oven, El Bolillo, and Red Dessert Dive offer three different reasons to stop: pastries and bread, Mexican pan dulce, and a dessert case worth saving room for. Each has a reported Google rating above four stars.
## Angela’s Oven: A Bakery Morning on Aurora Street

204 Aurora St., Houston, TX 77008 · 4.7★

Start at Angela’s Oven when you want your bakery stop to include breakfast. The original Aurora Street location serves pastries alongside breakfast and lunch, with coffee available throughout its opening hours. An almond croissant is a good place to begin; Google reviewers also mention the quiche and blueberry Danish.

There’s a reason to return beyond the morning pastry run: the bakery advertises Tuesday and Wednesday pizza nights at its original location. For this visit, though, keep it simple. Choose something from the pastry selection and leave time to enjoy it.

[Website](https://www.angelas-oven.com/) · [Menu](https://www.angelas-oven.com/gallery) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Angelas+Oven+204+Aurora+St+Houston+TX)
## El Bolillo: Pan Dulce to Bring Home

2517 Airline Dr., Houston, TX 77009 · 4.6★

El Bolillo’s roots are on Airline Drive, where the business began as a small Mexican bakery in 1998. Today, its selection spans traditional sweet breads, savory breads, pastries, and tres leches cakes.

For a first visit, try a concha and a pineapple empanada, then add a few bolillos for sandwiches at home. If you’re bringing dessert to dinner, explore the tres leches options. This is the stop to choose when you’d rather take home an assortment than settle on just one treat.

[Website](https://www.elbolillo.com/) · [Menu](https://order.toasttab.com/online/el-bolillo-bakery-heights-2517-airline-drive/item-album-10-3l_3d00b69e-c4bf-4e44-b149-bd71db3b0376) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=El+Bolillo+2517+Airline+Dr+Houston+TX)
## Red Dessert Dive: Save Room for Something Chocolate

1045 Studewood St., Houston, TX 77008 · 4.5★

Red Dessert Dive makes the decision between a cookie and a brownie pleasantly unnecessary. Its Debusker combines a dark chocolate brownie with an M&M cookie. Other options include salted caramel brownies, banana pudding, and bread pudding served with bourbon caramel sauce.

Prefer a morning visit? The menu also lists butter, chocolate, and almond croissants, plus quiche and scones. Choose Red when dessert is the main event, and check the day’s selection for seasonal flavors.

[Website](https://reddessertdive.com/) · [Menu](https://reddessertdive.com/menu/) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Red+Dessert+Dive+1045+Studewood+St+Houston+TX)

Planning your stop? Choose Angela’s Oven for breakfast and pastries, El Bolillo for a box to share, or Red Dessert Dive for brownies and pudding. Angela’s Oven and Red Dessert Dive list Monday closures, so plan those visits for another day.
