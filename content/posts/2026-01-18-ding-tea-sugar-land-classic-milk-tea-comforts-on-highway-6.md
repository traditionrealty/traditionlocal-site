Ding Tea Sugar Land delivers exactly what you want from a reliable bubble tea stop: consistent drinks, quality tapioca, and a cozy environment tucked into a busy plaza off Highway 6. With an H-E-B across the street and several eateries nearby, it’s an easy detour for a refreshing pick-me-up during Houston’s warm seasons. The menu spans classic milk teas, fruity lattes, Yakult-based drinks, and more—each customizable in sweetness and ice level. Their tapioca stands out: firm, chewy, and balanced in sweetness, pairing especially well with their signature honey milk tea.

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jan 6, 2020):** “This is a great choice if you're looking for quality milk tea with tapioca. The environment is warm, creative, and cozy—classic Sugar Land vibes right off Highway 6. Ding Tea offers milk teas, lattes, juices, and yogurt-based Yakult drinks, all perfect for our mostly warm seasons.

The tapioca is firm and chewy, not too sweet, and pairs beautifully with the honey milk tea. I like their rounded serving cups too—smaller and easier to sip without feeling overly full. I always ask for half ice and half sweetened, and they got it just right. Service was friendly and quick. Ding Tea is pretty popular across the U.S., and I can see why. I’ll definitely drop by again to try more items off the menu. But when in doubt, choose the classic milk tea!”
