***Category:*** *International Grocery* 

***Location:*** *Westchase, Houston* 

Seiwa Market is a tidy, well-organized specialty grocer focused entirely on Japanese products. Though smaller in size, the shelves are packed with unique imported items—from sauces and pantry staples to snacks, drinks, and specialty treats you won’t find in mainstream grocery stores. Prices can be steep due to import costs, but the selection appeals to anyone who enjoys niche Japanese ingredients or likes exploring international markets.

### **What to Expect**

- **Spotless aisles** and a compact but curated selection
- **Japanese pantry staples** including Kewpie mayo, dressings, sauces, and snacks
- **Hit-or-miss produce**, though the store keeps everything neatly displayed
- **Ready-to-drink beverages** like canned coffees, teas, and tapioca drinks
- **A small fresh-food section**, typically minimal depending on time of day

### **Must-Try Items**

- **Bourbon chocolate biscuits** – crisp, rich, and addictive
- **Wasabi rice crackers**
- **Sangaria Royal Milk Tea** – clean, lightly sweet, simple ingredients
- **Japanese pantry staples** (Kewpie mayo, dressings, curry mixes, sauces)

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Nov 4, 2021):**

 “I like this market! It’s a small specialty shop with exclusively Japanese products. Always clean, always organized, and never overly scented. The produce is hit or miss—cilantro was limp, but cherry tomatoes were vibrant. Prices can be a shock, especially for imported dressings and sauces.

The fresh food section was pretty bare when I visited, though I’m not sure I’d buy sushi here anyway. I love their chocolate bourbon biscuits—crisp and very chocolatey—as well as their savory rice crackers. I picked up wasabi ones this time. They carry plenty of ready-to-drink coffees and teas; my favorite is Sangaria Royal Milk Tea, lightly sweet and made with simple ingredients.

This plaza is quiet and tucked away, but worth the stop if you like browsing international markets. Support local!”
