Tucked inside a row of unassuming office-style buildings near Chimney Rock, Java Pura is one of Houston’s most meaningful hidden gems. This isn’t a café—it’s a working roastery, a coffee lab, and a hub for true bean obsessives. You’ll spot their beans at Urban Harvest Farmers Market, Central Market, and at catered events across the city. Visiting in person feels a bit like stepping behind the curtain into the scientific and artistic core of Houston’s coffee culture.

Here, freshness is everything. The team roasts beans onsite, packages them with care, and pours genuine passion into every batch. You won’t find latte art, but you *will* find people eager to talk origins, roast levels, equipment, and method. If you’re a home brewer ready to upgrade your morning ritual, this place is a must-stop during business hours.

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jan 29, 2020):**

“A gem of a coffee roaster headquartered in Gulfton, near Chimney Rock. It’s a bit difficult to find—just a row of plain office buildings—but that adds to the adventure of locating this coffee lab.

Java Pura is the local soul of the best coffee beans in Houston. You’ll find their products at Urban Harvest farmers market, Central Market, or at various catered events. This isn’t a coffee shop, so don’t expect a barista or latte art. Instead, you’ll meet folks who are incredibly passionate about their beans. They’re friendly and will offer a tour if you’re interested.

I visited a friend here and loved seeing their machines and products up close. Beans are roasted in-house, carefully packaged, and shipped across the city. You can purchase beans here during business hours—fresh, aromatic, and full of character. Grind at home to preserve that flavor.

Java Pura truly represents what it means to support local. Their heart shows through their beans, their packaging, and their dedication to the craft. Once you make the switch, you’ll never go back to Folgers. What’s in your cup?”
