## A Refreshing Alternative

Located in the Houston Pavilions, Caffè Bene sits in the heart of downtown, surrounded by shops and entertainment. It offers a different atmosphere compared to standard chain coffee shops, making it a fun change of pace.

## What to Try

### Frozen and Iced Beverages

These are my favorites, especially during Houston’s long and hot seasons. The drinks are refreshing and flavorful, offering a cool break from the heat.

### Gelato

Creamy and satisfying. A nice addition if you are in the mood for something sweet alongside your coffee.

### Honey Toast and Waffles

Caffè Bene is known for its dessert offerings. Honey toast bread and waffles are popular menu items and make for a shareable treat.

## Overall Impression

Caffè Bene brings an international café concept to Downtown Houston. With its Korean inspired menu, refreshing beverages, and dessert options, it stands apart from more traditional coffee chains.

If you are spending time at the Houston Pavilions or exploring downtown, it is a great spot to relax with a cold drink and something sweet.
