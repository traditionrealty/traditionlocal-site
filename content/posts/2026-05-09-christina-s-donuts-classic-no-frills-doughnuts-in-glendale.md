Sometimes the best food stops are the unexpected ones. Christina’s Donuts in **Glendale, CA** was exactly that — a glowing storefront spotted from across the street that practically called us in.

Late-night donut cravings can be risky (many shops sell out early), but to our surprise, the display case was still well stocked. If you’re looking for **classic, affordable doughnuts in Glendale**, this neighborhood shop keeps things simple — and satisfying.

## What We Ordered

I went straight for my three favorites:

### 🍎 Apple Fritter

Crisp on the outside with soft, sweet apple pockets inside. A dependable classic.

### 🍩 Cake Donut

Simple, slightly dense, and satisfying without being overly sugary.

### 🌀 Cruller

Light, airy texture with just the right amount of glaze.

All three were fresh, pleasantly crisp, and modest in size — which worked perfectly given the low prices.

## Price Point

The total came out to around **$3.30 for three donuts** — incredibly affordable by Southern California standards. The smaller size didn’t bother me at all given the value.

## The Atmosphere

Christina’s Donuts is straightforward and unpretentious — no trendy décor or elaborate flavors. Just standard donuts done right.

Service was efficient. The gentleman behind the counter appeared focused and working quickly. No frills — just fast and functional.

## Menu Notes

Beyond donuts, they also offer:

- Sandwiches
- Juice and smoothies
- Cold drinks

That said, the donuts are the main attraction.
