***[PERMANENTLY CLOSED - Store No Longer Operating]*** 

Randalls on Hwy 6 is one of Sugar Land’s longstanding neighborhood staples—familiar, comfortable, and refreshingly manageable in size. Though the storefront recently received a facelift and the interior has been lightly renovated, the store still carries a nostalgic charm. It’s a place where the layout feels intuitive, the aisles aren’t overwhelming, and you can be in and out without battling crowds or maze-like mega-markets.

This location features a bakery, a pharmacy, a Starbucks kiosk, and even an in-store option to renew your car registration—surprisingly convenient. The frozen food aisles are extensive, the dairy section is well-stocked with both local and national brands, and fresh flowers and balloons make it easy to grab last-minute celebration items. Cleanliness and friendly cashiers make the shopping experience smooth and familiar.

### **Highlights**

- Neighborhood-sized grocery store (easy in-and-out)
- Bakery, pharmacy & Starbucks kiosk
- Car registration renewal available onsite
- Fresh flowers, balloons & celebration essentials
- Great selection of frozen foods, dairy & local brands
- Clean store & consistently friendly staff

### **Review**

**Sara I. (May 20, 2020):** 

“This Randalls has been around my neighborhood forever. Conveniently located on Hwy 6, it’s gotten a facelift over the years, though walking inside still feels nostalgic—like stepping back in time.

It’s always clean, the crowd is diverse, and the cashiers are friendly. They remind you to use your Randalls card for savings. I love that the store isn’t huge like the nearby H-E-B; you can find what you need and be out quickly.

I’ve picked up great cheese, ice cream, and milk here, and the frozen section is impressively stocked. With a pharmacy, bakery, and Starbucks kiosk—and even the option to renew your car registration—it’s surprisingly convenient. Thanks for your neighborhood presence, my old friend.”
