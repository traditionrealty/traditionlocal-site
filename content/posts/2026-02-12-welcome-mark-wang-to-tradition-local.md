### *Celebrating Houston’s Food & Travel Voice*

![](/media/d2d48b_fafc0dcbb0744f138b182ae1f081a3fb~mv2.jpg)

We’re excited to officially welcome **our good friend Mark Wang** to the Tradition Local family!

Mark is a longtime Houston food and travel writer whose work has helped locals discover hidden gems, neighborhood favorites, and unforgettable destinations both in our city and beyond. With years of experience covering Houston’s ever-evolving culinary scene, Mark brings insight, authenticity, and a true appreciation for the stories behind the places we love.

Houston is one of the most diverse food cities in the country, and Mark has spent years capturing its flavor from hole-in-the-wall legends to chef-driven concepts pushing creative boundaries. His passion for travel also adds a broader perspective, connecting local culture to global inspiration in a way that resonates with readers.

Today, we’re proud to celebrate **Mark’s inaugural review and first official post with Tradition Local.** His debut piece sets the tone for what’s ahead: thoughtful storytelling, honest reviews, and a deep respect for the people and businesses that make Houston special.

Mark, congratulations on your first post and welcome aboard. We’re honored to have you contribute your voice and expertise to our growing community.

To our readers, this is just the beginning. Stay tuned for more food discoveries, travel insights, and insider perspectives from Mark as we continue highlighting the best of Houston together.

Welcome to the family, Mark!

**Sara Loren**
