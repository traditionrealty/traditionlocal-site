Buc-ee’s in Richmond may be one of the smaller outposts of the iconic Texas chain, but it still delivers the charm, convenience, and quick-stop satisfaction locals adore. This location is built for speedy in-and-out visits—perfect for grabbing a hot drink, stocking up on road-trip snacks, or fueling up before heading back toward Sugar Land or the surrounding areas.

Despite its compact size, the essentials are all here: hot coffee, cold bottled drinks, deli bites, baked goods, and everyday necessities like milk and eggs. The attached gas station and car wash make it a reliable one-stop shop, even if it doesn’t boast the massive retail section found in their flagship stores.

### **Must-Try / Must-Grab Items**

- Piping hot Buc-ee’s coffee (strong & smooth)
- Banana pudding cups
- Fresh deli snacks & baked goods
- Assorted cold bottled beverages
- Quick-access essentials (milk, eggs, grab-and-go treats)

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Feb 4, 2022):** 

“This is just a simple and quick grab n’ go Buc-ee’s. If you're randomly craving their in-store snacks and treats, you'll find that here. They have the usual assortment of hot coffee, deli items and baked goods. I like their coffee—strong, piping hot, and far from bitter.

Granted, this location is much smaller than their other storefronts, but many of their products are still available. It has fewer bells and whistles though; for souvenirs or gifts, you’ll need to visit a larger Buc-ee’s. This one has an attached gas station and car wash just like the others. I’ve heard good things about their car wash, so I’ll definitely revisit.

I grabbed my favorite banana pudding and some coffee. Service was quick and simple. With plenty of beverages and random necessities like milk and eggs, it’s a handy little spot not far from Sugar Land. Parking is a bit tight, but again, this is totally a grab-and-go location. Toodle-oo!”
