HTeaO brings a refreshing, playful twist to classic Southern iced tea. This bright, easygoing shop offers a full spectrum of chilled teas—from bold black blends to fruity infusions—allowing you to mix, match, and build your perfect drink. With self-serve dispensers, mountains of crunchy pebble ice, and clean ingredient lists posted up front, the space feels like a build-your-own-bevvie playground. Add in a drive-thru, fruit add-ins, and fun branded merch, and you’ve got a go-to stop for warm Texas days.

### **Must-Try Items:**

- Black Tea with Mint (sweet or unsweet)
- Ryan Palmer (tea + pink lemonade blend)
- Unsweetened black teas for custom mixes
- Fresh fruit add-ins
- Dry tea blends for home brewing

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Mar 20, 2022):** “HTeaO on the go! This cool little spot has a large variety of chilled teas—simple & classic Southern-style flavors ranging from fruity to deep black teas. I'm a huge fan of iced tea, and this place lets you customize anything. It’s basically a little playground full of flavors to mix & match.

My favorite was the black tea with mint—crisp, refreshing, and sweet. I mixed the sweet version with a bit of unsweetened for balance. Most blends seem clean in ingredients, and they even sell dry tea if you want to brew at home. They’ve got fruit cups too if you want to toss fresh fruit into your drink.

Cute gear at the front—tea shirts and caps. (Yes, *tea* shirts!) With the drive-thru and all the choices, I’ll definitely be back. Grab a cup and head to Oyster Creek Park for a walk… you’ll thank me later.”
