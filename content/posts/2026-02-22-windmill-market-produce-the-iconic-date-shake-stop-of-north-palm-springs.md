There are road trip treats—and then there are **Palm Springs date shakes**, the kind that feel like a rite of passage when traveling through the desert. Windmill Market & Produce, nestled among the iconic spinning turbines of North Palm Springs, serves one of the most memorable versions you’ll taste: thick, creamy, aromatic, and deeply satisfying.

Made with **fresh locally grown dates**, their classic vanilla date shake is rich in flavor with warm caramel notes that taste almost like brown sugar melting into cold cream. The texture is luscious and velvety, and the dates practically dissolve on the tongue. It’s the perfect refreshment after exploring Joshua Tree or cruising through the Yucca Valley.

Beyond shakes, the market sells beautifully packaged fresh dates—ideal for snacking or gifting. The coconut-rolled dates are a standout, offering a sweet, tropical twist. Friendly and efficient service adds to the charm, making it an easy and comforting stop during a desert drive.

Windmill Market is a slice of local flavor infused with a sense of place—surrounded by towering windmills, sunbaked mountains, and all the nostalgic vibes of a classic California road trip.

**Must-Try Items:**

- Vanilla ice cream *fresh date shake* (the original, the icon)
- Coconut-rolled dates
- Other fresh date varieties to take home

## **Review**

Sara I. (Mar 3, 2023):

 “The fresh date shake here was marvelous.. thick in viscosity, creamy and out of this world. The aroma reminded me of brown sugar and the flavor was warm, even though it was a cold and creamy milkshake. They use fresh dates that are commonly grown near and around the palm springs area.

We didn't get to venture too deep within Palm Springs, but this was the closest market to us when we were venturing out of Joshua Tree. Folks were friendly and efficient with their service. We opted for the vanilla ice cream date shake, which is the original flavor here. The dates just melt in your mouth and are totally otherworldly, along with the fresh packaged dates that you can purchase here. They make for a great gift as well, and there are plenty of different flavors — my favorite being the dates rolled in dessicated coconut.

All in all, this is a pretty neat spot. I'd love to revisit and try their savory foods and other items as well. 

Geographically, it's super cool to be surrounded by windmills nearby, literally and figuratively. When around and passing through the Yucca Valley, you can count on Windmill Market & Produce for a great beverage and perhaps a snack or two while cruising through your road trip. :) Until next time!”
