WEST on WEST—affectionately known as **WoW**—has quickly transformed from an empty lot into one of Westchase’s most vibrant up-and-coming shopping plazas. What was once a quiet patch of land in 2023 is now a lively hub filling up with boutiques, cafés, playful attractions, and bright, nostalgic energy. It’s the kind of place that keeps evolving, making each visit a little different from the last.

With new shops popping up regularly and colorful, family-friendly stops like TESO Life bringing 90s-inspired fun, WoW is becoming a weekend stroll favorite. Claw machines glow with neon charm, Sanrio characters make surprise appearances, and there’s a sense of joyful exploration woven into the experience. Whether you're running errands, meeting a friend, or simply wandering, this plaza delivers a cheerful dose of nostalgia and discovery.

**Why You’ll Love It:**

- Vibrant mix of boutiques, cafés, and specialty shops
- TESO Life with fun 90s-style claw machines
- Family-friendly atmosphere with kid-approved activities
- A still-growing plaza worth revisiting as new vendors move in

## **Review**

Sara I. (Aug 30, 2025): 

“I remember when Mark pointed out WoW plaza back in 2023... it was an empty lot. We were hanging out that day, running on some Trader Joe's errands. Lol.

Fast forward and I have had the chance to visit WoW twice so far... earlier in the year and this week.

It was fun.

The plaza continues to fill up with shoppes, boutiques and cafes. I visited TESO Life earlier in the year and brought back bébé with me. He loved the bright claw machines which have been all the rage this summer season. Hello 90's vibrancy and excitement. No Labubu for us, we're good. We were happy to hang out with Pekkle the duck instead... Sanrio for life. Again… cue the 90s vibes. Thank you for joining in on the adventure this week.... Both Mark and I are 16 years Yelp Elite... And it's always fun to chat about the city and the latest scoop of what goes on in town.

Looking forward to revisiting WoW - West on West, when more shops open and pop up!”
