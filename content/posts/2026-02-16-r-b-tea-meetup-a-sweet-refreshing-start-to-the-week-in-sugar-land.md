The **R&B Tea Community Meetup** in Sugar Land was a bright and flavorful start to the week, offering delicious boba beverages and warm hospitality. Known for their high-quality drinks and consistently friendly service, this R&B Tea location proved to be the perfect spot for a relaxed afternoon treat—especially on a warm Texas Monday.

With a menu full of customizable drinks, guests had plenty of options to explore. The standout order of the day was a **strawberry matcha fresh milk tea with tapioca**, balanced at 70% sweetness for the ideal blend of earthy matcha and bright fruitiness. As always, R&B’s tapioca pearls delivered that signature chewy bite and rich flavor, making every sip satisfying.

**Event Highlights:**

- Friendly, attentive staff (shoutout to Brandon & Julia)
- Strawberry matcha fresh milk tea with perfectly chewy tapioca
- Great customization options for sweetness & mix-ins
- Consistent beverage quality and flavorful milk teas
- A fun community gathering on a warm afternoon
- Ideal Monday pick-me-up with caffeine + comfort

## **Review**

Sara I. (May 20, 2024):

“It was great to visit this spot for community week! I didn't even know community week was a thing and thought this was an ‘elite week’ sorta deal. Nope! More boba tea for all!

R&B has some tasty beverage choices and it was not my first time visiting this spot within the land of Sugar. Quality of beverages and customer service is spot on. Brandon and Julia were friendly and answered any of the questions I had in mind. I ordered a fresh milk beverage (strawberry matcha tea with tapioca). Asked for half sweet, but Brandon mentioned the matcha is quite strong and bitter. Bumped it to 70% sweetness and it was perfect! R&B tapioca is always chewy with the right texture and flavor. All in all, the matcha blended well with the bright flavors of strawberry. Will definitely reorder this next time! Their milk teas in general are great. Plenty of options for flavors, mixins and toppings.

This was a great spot to visit on a warm Monday afternoon. Getting the week started with a tasty caffeinated fix!

 Until next time! SYOY. :)”
