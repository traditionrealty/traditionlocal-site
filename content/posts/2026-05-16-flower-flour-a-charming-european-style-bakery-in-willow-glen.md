Tucked into the heart of **Willow Glen in San Jose**, Flower Flour is the kind of neighborhood bakery that stops you in your tracks. With its whimsical mural exterior and European-inspired concept of pairing **flowers and cakes**, this sweet little gem feels straight out of a Parisian side street.

If you’re searching for:

- Best bakery in Willow Glen
- San Jose patisserie
- Crepes in San Jose
- European-style bakery near me

Flower Flour deserves a visit.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Beautiful mural storefront
- Fresh pastries and custom cakes
- Savory and sweet crepes
- Flowers sold alongside baked goods
- Cozy neighborhood atmosphere

## First Impressions

The outside mural — full of cakes and charming details — immediately draws you in. The concept of selling bouquets alongside pastries feels delightfully European. Who wouldn’t want to pick up fresh flowers and a cake in one stop?

Inside, the display cases sparkle with petite cakes and pastries, and the breakfast and lunch menus offer both sweet and savory options.

## What I Ordered

### 🥞 Savory Vegetarian Crepe

A pleasant surprise — and easily one of the best crepes I’ve had.

- Lightly sweet yet savory
- Filled with roasted vegetables
- Melted cheese throughout
- Perfectly balanced flavor

It was hearty, satisfying, and didn’t miss the presence of meat at all. A beautifully executed vegetarian option.

### ☕ Caffè Latte

While enjoyable, the latte wasn’t the strongest part of the visit. It paired well with the meal but didn’t quite stand out.

## What to Try Next Time

- Morning buns (sold out by afternoon — always a good sign)
- Custom cakes
- Petite pastries

There’s plenty left to explore.

## The Atmosphere

Flower Flour has a warm, inviting presence in a quiet neighborhood. It feels personal, curated, and thoughtfully designed — a lovely place to sit, sip, and savor.

## Overall Impression

Flower Flour blends bakery charm with European-inspired aesthetics, offering both beautiful visuals and satisfying flavors. The savory crepe alone makes it worth the visit — and the concept of flowers + pastries makes it memorable.

If you’re strolling through Willow Glen, stop in. You might leave with a bouquet and a slice of cake.
