Tucked along **Westheimer Road in Houston**, Old Saigon Café offers a fresh and flavorful take on Vietnamese cuisine in a clean, welcoming setting. Surrounded by a busy strip of neighboring restaurants, this cozy café stands out for its warm hospitality, vibrant flavors, and a few unexpected menu surprises.

On a special New Year’s Eve dinner, the experience felt nothing short of magical — thoughtful recommendations, beautifully presented dishes, and balanced flavors that left a lasting impression.

## What to Order

### 🌿 Artichoke Appetizer (Owner’s Recommendation)

A rare find at most Vietnamese cafés. The artichokes were beautifully plated and dressed simply with lemon and olive oil. Tender, meaty, and refreshing — a bright and unexpected starter that set the tone for the meal.

### 🌶 Spicy Chicken Lemongrass Banh Mi

A standout dish. The bread was fresh and crispy, delivering that essential crunch every great banh mi needs.

The shredded chicken was saucy, spicy, and layered with lemongrass flavor. The heat was noticeable but balanced — different from the traditional cold-cut banh mi and refreshingly bold.

Served in a basket alongside warm, crispy French fries, this sandwich felt both comforting and elevated.

### 🥭 Crab & Mango Soft Rolls

Light, refreshing, and unique. Stuffed with crab and sweet mango, these soft rolls offered a sweet-savory contrast that worked beautifully.

Served with both classic **nuoc cham** and peanut dipping sauces, they made for a delicate yet satisfying plate.

## On the List for Next Time

The **tableside pho** is calling — especially during cooler weather. Watching a steaming bowl assembled tableside sounds like the perfect cozy dining experience.

## The Atmosphere

Old Saigon Café is simple and clean, with a welcoming vibe that makes you feel immediately comfortable. Service throughout the evening was warm, thoughtful, and genuinely attentive — the kind of hospitality that turns a meal into a memorable night.
