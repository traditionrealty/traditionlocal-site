Nestled in the heart of **Los Feliz, Los Angeles**, The Guest House blends cocktail bar energy with cozy coffeehouse charm. With its home-style furnishings and relaxed neighborhood setting, it’s the kind of spot that draws in creatives, locals, and weekend loungers alike.

Part café, part cocktail lounge, and part event space, The Guest House aims to be a versatile gathering place in one of LA’s coolest neighborhoods.

If you’re searching for:

- Coffee shop in Los Feliz
- Cozy café Los Angeles
- Cocktail bar Los Feliz
- Brunch and coffee near Griffith Park

The Guest House is worth a casual visit.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Cozy, home-style furnishings
- Located in trendy Los Feliz
- Coffee, tea, cocktails, and pastries
- Refillable tea
- Relaxed neighborhood vibe

## The Atmosphere

The space leans heavily into a curated, hip aesthetic — warm wood tones, vintage-inspired décor, and lounge-style seating. It feels like stepping into someone’s eclectic living room.

While the vibe may skew slightly hipster, the cozy layout makes it comfortable for catching up with friends or enjoying a solo coffee break.

## What to Order

### ☕ Coffee

Smooth and well-prepared — a solid choice if you’re just stopping in for caffeine.

### 🍵 Tea (Refill Friendly)

Tea is served in small mugs, but refills are available, which makes it a practical and appreciated touch.

### 🍰 Cakes & Pastries

The pastry selection is appealing and pairs nicely with coffee or tea. A good option if you’re craving something sweet in the afternoon.

## Price Point

The Guest House leans toward the pricier side, especially considering the casual café atmosphere. That said, you’re paying partly for the neighborhood and ambiance.

## Overall Impression

The Guest House delivers a cozy setting with solid coffee and pleasant pastries in a desirable Los Feliz location. While prices run slightly high and the aesthetic leans trendy, it remains a comfortable neighborhood café for a relaxed afternoon.

If you’re strolling through Los Feliz and need a warm drink in a stylish setting, this spot fits the bill.
