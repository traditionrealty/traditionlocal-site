Located in the heart of **Sugar Land, TX**, Aki Steak & Sushi is a neighborhood-friendly spot offering a wide-ranging menu that spans **Japanese, sushi, Chinese dishes, and steakhouse favorites**. For suburban dining, it’s the kind of versatile restaurant that works well for groups and families with different cravings.

One standout feature? **All-day Sunday happy hour specials** — a rare and welcome find.

## Sunday Happy Hour

Sunday specials run all day, making it easy to enjoy discounted sushi without rushing in for a narrow time window.

One of the biggest highlights:

### 🍣 12 Pieces of Sushi for $8

A true bargain. The rolls were thinner than average but fresh, simple, and satisfying — especially at that price point.

## What We Ordered

### 🍜 Singapore Noodles with Chicken

We split this dish along with sushi. Traditionally, Singapore noodles lean salty and curry-forward. In this case, the dish was mild and slightly under-seasoned.

We added soy sauce to boost the flavor — something we don’t usually prefer to do. A stronger curry note would have elevated the dish.

### 🍣 Sushi

Simple, fresh, and a solid value during happy hour. Not overly elaborate — but dependable.

## Other Menu Options

Aki isn’t just about sushi. The menu also includes:

- Steak dishes

It’s ideal for groups where everyone wants something different.

## Atmosphere & Service

The restaurant draws plenty of local families, which adds to its relaxed suburban feel. Servers were kind and attentive, and the food arrived promptly.

Most importantly — everything felt fresh, and there were no post-sushi regrets (always a plus).

## Overall Impression

Aki Steak & Sushi may not deliver mind-blowing flavors, but it offers **solid, fresh food at fair prices**, especially during Sunday happy hour. The diverse menu makes it a reliable option in Sugar Land for casual dinners with friends or family.

If you’re in the area and craving sushi without breaking the bank, Aki is a practical and pleasant choice.
