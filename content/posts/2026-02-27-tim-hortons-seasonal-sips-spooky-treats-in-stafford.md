Step into the cozy, quick-stop charm of Tim Hortons in Stafford—a compact corner café near Meadows Place that delivers big on seasonal flavor. Though the interior is small and snug, the drive-thru keeps things moving with quick, efficient service and friendly staff. This is classic commercial coffee done right: reliable, flavorful, and refreshingly unpretentious.

October brings out the magic here. From pumpkin spice delights to their fun, festive Reese’s Peanut Butter collaboration drinks, Tim Hortons leans fully into spooky-season joy. Their Halloween TimBits buckets are a treat for both kids and adults, and the selection of pastries—from the crisp spinach artichoke puff to their beloved honey donut—never disappoint. Whether you’re craving a refreshing iced mocha or a creamy hot latte, this spot makes for an easy, satisfying grab-and-go stop on your way into the city.

**Must-Try Items:**

- Halloween TimBits Bucket
- Reese’s PB seasonal drinks
- Pumpkin Spice lattes and coffees
- Iced Mocha
- Honey Donut & Spinach Artichoke Puff Pastry

#### **Reviews**

 Sara I. (Oct 7, 2025):

 “Halloween spooky flavors are here.. such as their collab with anything Reeses PB flavors and all things autumnal pumpkin spice. They also have Halloween TimBits buckets for all things trick or treat. Oh my, I am loving it and Tim Hortons has always been a favorite grab and go coffee spot for me. This location is a bit small and cramped, tucked into a little corner near meadows place. It is definitely a smaller location but I typically visit their drive thru. Their service is quick and efficient, no complaints. Coffee is good, products are good.. there's nothing else to say. Its commercial coffee given with prompt service. I definitely like it better than that other overhyped place with the green and white logo. Their iced mocha is refreshing and their hot lattes are creamy and flavorful. Their honey donut is yummy and the spinach artichoke puffy pastry is crispy and buttery. The toddler loves it and we visit every now and then. This location is a quick jump into the big city. We will probably revisit and grab a timbits halloween bucket when given a chance! Until next time. xoxo ~”
