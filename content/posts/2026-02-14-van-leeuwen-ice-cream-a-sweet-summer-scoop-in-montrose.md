***Category:*** *Ice Cream & Frozen Yogurt, Desserts* 

***Location:*** *Montrose, Houston* 

Bright, cheerful, and sweetly satisfying, Van Leeuwen in Montrose is a delightful stop for anyone craving premium ice cream with creative flair. Located within the Montrose Collective, this airy, modern scoop shop is known for its ultra-creamy textures, inventive flavors, and clean, welcoming interior. On a warm summer afternoon, the easy flow of service and the friendly staff make this spot shine.

Guests can sample flavors using metal tasting spoons (a fun, eco-friendly twist), then settle in for a treat inside the cozy seating area. Whether you're mixing flavors, indulging in a sundae, or sipping on a nostalgic soda, Van Leeuwen serves up simple joys in every scoop.

**Must-Try Items:**

- Coffee Affogato Ice Cream
- Dark Chocolate Brownie Ice Cream
- Texas Rodeo Sundae
- Root Beer Soda

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jun 18, 2022):**

“Loved this little sweet elite perk! This ice cream spot is bright and cheery, perfect on this sunny Saturday visit. I've been to V.L. before, and the establishment was just as nice and clean, well organized like last time. The lines were non existent, so Lou and I grabbed our ice cream and got to comfortably sit indoors.

The scooper was friendly and allowed us to try a few samples. I went for the coffee affogato flavor and the dark chocolate brownie flavor. Out of the two, the affogato was creamier and lush with a nice light coffee flavor. The brownie ice cream was a bit on the drier side but still tasty.

We also tried the Texas rodeo sundae and root beer soda. Flavors were great—dreamy, creamy, crunchy, and nostalgic. Overall we had a great time. Until next time!”
