Verbena Coffee is a warm, wood-toned retreat tucked into the Memorial/Energy Corridor area—a neighborhood café that has quickly grown into a beloved local staple. With Scandinavian-inspired interiors, thoughtfully crafted drinks, and collaborations with nearby bakeries and pop-ups, it’s the kind of space that invites you to slow down on a rainy morning or tuck in for a cozy afternoon recharge.

The drink menu leans comforting and aromatic, featuring house-made syrups, quality loose-leaf teas, and espresso beverages that highlight balance over excess sweetness. Whether you're a coffee devotee or a tea enthusiast, Verbena offers something crafted with intention.

### **What to Try**

- **London Fog Latte** – loose-leaf Earl Grey steeped in foamy milk with fragrant vanilla
- **Brown Sugar Latte** – lightly sweet, house-made syrup; add cinnamon for extra warmth
- **Cappuccino** – classic, well-balanced, and smooth
- **Local loaf cakes & croissants** – baked goods delivered fresh from nearby partners

### **Review**

**Sara I. (May 23, 2021):** 

“Such a great little coffee shop in the neighborhood, and it hasn’t even been open for two years. They’re thriving in the Memorial area and it’s wonderful supporting local. I love visiting independently owned coffee shoppes and trying the drinks they craft in house.

My favorite here is the London Fog latte—close to my heart, and not everyone makes it right. Their loose-leaf Earl Grey steeped in warm, foamy milk with fragrant vanilla is perfect. The bergamot is sharp and comforting, especially on a gray and rainy day.

Coffee drinks are just as good. The brown sugar latte, made with their house syrup, isn’t overly sweet—adding cinnamon makes the flavor pop. Cappuccinos are solid too. I’m looking forward to trying their sliced loaf cakes and croissants from a local bakery.

The interior is woodsy and tan, very Scandinavian. They also host pop-ups with other cafés like Koffeteria. Always something fun happening at this neighborhood spot. Until next time.”
