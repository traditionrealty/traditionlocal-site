This new Starbucks near Meadows Place brings a fresh, modern, and spotless vibe to a growing corner of Stafford. Tucked into the same plaza as Aldi, it’s an easy stop for a warm drink while running errands or passing through the area. The interior is clean, minimal, and inviting—exactly what you hope for in a newly opened café.

Service is friendly and efficient, and the baristas craft drinks with attention to freshness and flavor. Whether you’re grabbing something through the drive-thru or stepping inside for a quiet moment, this location stands out for its warmth and cleanliness.

### **What to Try**

- Café Misto (café au lait) with a hint of vanilla bean
- Classic hot coffees
- Seasonal warm beverages

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jan 10, 2022):** 

“I don't typically write reviews on Starbucks… but this is a brand-new location right by Meadows Place in Stafford. It’s in the same plaza as Aldi. This Starbucks is very clean, quaint, and minimal. Cleanliness is such a huge factor right now, and they completely ace it.

I walked inside rather than visiting the drive-thru. Staff was friendly and whipped me up a warm café au lait—with a small drop of vanilla bean flavoring. At SB, it’s called a café misto, so I keep that in mind. It’s probably the one drink I like getting here. They made it tall, frothy, and fresh. The beans didn’t taste sour or rancid.

When in the area grabbing groceries, I may revisit again. I’m glad this part of town is thriving—it makes the experience pleasant and comfortable. Until next time.”
