Tutti Frutti has been a Sugar Land classic for years, one of the last remaining self-serve froyo spots in the area. Known for its colorful interior and mix-and-match toppings, it continues to draw in families, students, and longtime regulars. Recently, the shop added bubble waffles and smoothies—bringing a fresh twist to its familiar lineup. While the flavors have softened a bit in tartness over time, the concept remains nostalgic, simple, and sweet.

#### **What to Order**

- **Bubble Waffle with Frozen Yogurt** – Warm, fun, and extra satisfying with toppings.
- **Classic Tart Froyo Cup** – Customizable with fruit, candy, or crunchy add-ins.
- **Fresh Fruit Smoothie** – A newer menu addition that pairs well with warm weather.

#### **Reviews**

**Sara I. (Mar 16, 2019):**

“Surprise surprise… they are now serving bubble waffles and smoothies here. Smelled delightful upon entering this bright spot. Seems as if they've gotten really busy over time. Flavors are a bit too mellow now and not tart enough. I do appreciate the fact that new items are on the menu. Change is good. I do feel as if Tutti Frutti needs to update their yogurt flavors and introduce new flavors after featuring the same ones after all these years (ie: Yogurt Land). Still a nice concept and one of the last fro yo spots remaining around the Sugar Land area.”

**Sara I. (Sep 23, 2011):**

“I love the fact that there are fruity and non-fruity flavours to choose from at Tutti Frutti. I recently checked out this establishment on a hot afternoon day. Tutti Frutti is a self-serve frozen yogurt bar where you choose from a variety of flavours and add any combination of toppings you like. The interior is cute and colorful. I also love the fact that there isn’t any blaring music—sometimes I would get a headache leaving Berripop.

The flavours are fresh and super cold. I noticed the yogurt was freezing but didn’t mind because it didn’t end up melting.

To top it off, you get a stamp card. After the eighth visit, you receive a free 16 oz cup of yogurt! This sweet deal will definitely make me dive back into their yogurt. :)”
