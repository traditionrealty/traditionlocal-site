Located along Hillcroft in Houston’s Gulfton area, **Alpha Dessert Juice Café** (formerly known to many as Agha Juice) is a South Asian dessert spot known for its rich kulfi, falooda, and culturally inspired sweet treats. With a recently expanded interior featuring murals and photographs celebrating Pakistani history and culture, the café brings a vibrant identity to the neighborhood.

If you’re searching for:

- Best kulfi in Houston
- Pakistani ice cream near me
- Falooda in Gulfton
- Halal dessert café Houston

Alpha Dessert Juice Café is worth a visit — especially on a hot day.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Authentic Pakistani kulfi
- Cultural murals and décor
- Halal-friendly menu
- Variety of South Asian desserts
- Generous portions

## The Atmosphere

The café has expanded and now features colorful murals and imagery representing Pakistani culture and history. It adds personality and authenticity to the space — a thoughtful touch that reflects the roots of the desserts being served.

## Must-Try: Kulfi

### 🍨 Kulfi (Pakistani Ice Cream)

You truly can’t go wrong here.

- Thick and creamy texture
- Made with condensed milk
- Intensely sweet
- Rich and satisfying

Available either on a stick or chopped into pieces and served on a plate.

The texture is denser than traditional ice cream — almost fudge-like — making it a standout dessert.

## Other Menu Options

Alpha Dessert Juice Café also offers:

- Falooda (a layered South Asian dessert drink)
- South Asian-inspired milkshakes
- Burgers and savory snacks
- Juice drinks

While I’m less familiar with their savory offerings, desserts are clearly the main attraction.

## Coffee Drink Experience

On a sweltering Houston afternoon, I ordered a cold coffee drink expecting something refreshing. Instead, it leaned more toward a milkshake — heavy with coffee ice cream, milk, and ice.

If you enjoy thick, creamy shakes, this may be for you. Personally, it felt too heavy and lacked the bold coffee flavor I was craving.

## Service Notes

During my visit, the drink preparation took longer than expected and required a reminder. Service may vary, but improved training and attentiveness would elevate the overall experience.

## Review

**Sara I. (Jul 21, 2016):**

> “You can't go wrong with their kulfi — thick and creamy.The flavor is super sweet because of the condensed milk. It’s better to stick to their kulfi, falooda, and South Asian inspired desserts.”

## Overall Impression

Alpha Dessert Juice Café shines when it leans into what it does best — traditional Pakistani desserts like kulfi and falooda. While not the go-to spot for coffee, it’s a solid choice for authentic South Asian sweets in Houston.

On a hot Texas day, a slice of kulfi may be all you need.
