Dunkin’ in Westchase delivers what it’s best known for: quick, reliable coffee and convenient drive-thru service. While the beverages tend to be consistent—especially their hot and iced coffee—the baked goods at this location can be hit-or-miss. With only a handful of Dunkin’ stores in Houston, this one aims to fill a much-needed niche, though it hasn’t quite matched the freshness level found in other states.

### **What to Expect**

- **Consistent coffee**, both hot and iced
- **Drive-thru convenience**, especially during morning rush
- **Pastries that vary in freshness**, depending on the time of day
- **Friendly, efficient service**

### **Must-Try Items**

- Iced Coffee with Pumpkin Spice (½ sweetness recommended)
- Hot Coffee (simple, crisp, reliable)
- Cake Donuts (better earlier in the day)

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Nov 2, 2021):**

 “Dunkin’ serves up some decent hot coffee—always crisp and tasty. Whether hot or iced, it’s a good alternative to the usual Starbucks run. I grabbed an iced coffee with half pumpkin spice flavoring today and paired it with a pumpkin spice cake donut. The donut wasn’t bad, but it wasn’t fresh either—oily to the touch and a bit artificial in taste.

This location doesn’t have the freshest donuts, which is disappointing since we don’t have many Dunkin’ shops in Houston. Service, however, has always been good, and the drive-thru is convenient. Funny enough, I recently tried a Dunkin’ in Alabama and their donuts were much fresher. Hoping Texas can step up its Dunkin’ game—otherwise, I’ll stick to Shipley’s when I want quality pastries.”
