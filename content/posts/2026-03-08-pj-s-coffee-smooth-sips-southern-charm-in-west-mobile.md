Nestled along Airport Boulevard in the Pinebrook Plaza—right beside Whole Foods—this PJ’s Coffee offers a refreshing, cozy retreat for coffee lovers in West Mobile. With a convenient drive-thru and a clean, calm interior, it strikes the perfect balance between grab-and-go speed and sit-and-sip comfort. Friendly staff, a well-kept space, and a menu full of Southern-inspired coffee classics make this location a much-needed addition to the area’s small café scene.

Whether you're stopping by for a morning boost or an afternoon cool-down, PJ’s brings that signature New Orleans–style richness to every cup.

### **Must-Try Items:**

- **Iced Café au Lait** – smooth, frothy, lightly sweetened perfection
- **Beignets** – warm, classic New Orleans-style treats (for your next visit!)
- **Fresh Pastries** – muffins, baked goods, and rotating seasonal offerings
- **Honey-sweetened beverages** – a great add-on to mellow bold brews

### **Reviews**

**Sara I. (Apr 28, 2022):** 

“Crisp, refreshing and sweet! I'm liking this location on Airport Blvd, in West Mobile. It's tucked in the same plaza as Whole Foods Market and a few other shops. This location has a drive thru and a lovely interior inside. Seating is limited but it's ample enough for this part of town. I absolutely adored the clean and quiet environment. Staff was friendly and helpful as well.

I decided upon a café au lait—but make it iced! Asked for some honey as well, and boy was it an awesome bevvie. The drink was frothy and refreshing. Just enough milk to cut through the sharp notes of coffee bean. I do like the flavors that PJ’s has to offer.

Hoping they can open more locations in the South. I'll definitely be back to try their beignets, muffins and a warm bevvie next time around. Happy this location opened up in the Pinebrook Plaza, especially with far and few cafés in the area. Hope it stays for good. Cheerios! Until next time. :)”
