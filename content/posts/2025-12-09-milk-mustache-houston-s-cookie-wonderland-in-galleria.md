Step into the sugary charm of Milk Mustache, a whimsical cookie shop tucked into Fountain View in the Galleria/Uptown area. This isn’t your average dessert spot—it’s a full sensory experience. As soon as you walk in, you’re wrapped in the warm aroma of buttery dough and nostalgic sweetness. With its colorful, cozy atmosphere and standout service, Milk Mustache is a must-visit for any dessert lover in Houston.

Their cookies are a true craft—crispy on the edges, gooey in the middle, and craggy in the best way possible. This local gem rotates in fresh flavors throughout the year, keeping things exciting with options like soft serve and dessert drinks. It’s a perfect stop for a quick treat, a post-lunch indulgence, or a sugary gift for a loved one.

**Must-Try Treats:**

- Warm, gooey cookies with rotating seasonal flavors
- Sweet dessert beverages and soft serve ice cream
- A cute, Instagram-worthy interior

#### **Review:**

**Sara I. (July 2, 2025):**

“Yummy cookies galore.

I have always liked this sweet gem of a spot. When you walk into milk mustache, you smell all things butter and sweetness. The aroma wafts in the cute little shoppe. It is every so colorful, cute and whimsical in here. I love this cozy yet nonchalant cookie shoppe right on fountain view. Milk Mustache honestly has some of the best cookies in the city. That says a lot, cause' we've got quite a few cookie/dessert spots in the city. But I'm picky and I truly believe these are some of the best cookies I've had in Houston. They are warm, crispy and yet gooey as well. They have that classic texture and flavor that a cookie should measure up to.. and they are crag like. I love how this spot has a few new flavors throughout the year and they also have dessert beverages and soft serve as well. Service is always nice and prompt. The dessert's here are quite sweet but full of flavor, which keeps me revisiting every now and then. Try it for yourself, you'll be in for a treat. Nothing dry and nothing boring about these cute cookies. Until next time!”
