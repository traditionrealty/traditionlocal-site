Tucked into a cozy corner near Nordstrom inside **The Galleria in Uptown Houston**, Cafe Dolce Gelato has long been a go-to stop for shoppers craving **Italian coffee, gelato, crepes, and paninis**. With its European café feel and longstanding presence in one of Houston’s busiest shopping destinations, it offers a convenient caffeine break in the midst of retail therapy.

While many visitors come for the gelato, this particular visit was all about the coffee — specifically, **Illy espresso**, one of Italy’s most beloved coffee brands.

If you’re searching for:

- Best coffee in The Galleria Houston
- Illy coffee Houston
- Gelato near Nordstrom Galleria
- Italian café Uptown Houston

Cafe Dolce Gelato is one of the classic stops inside the mall.

## Why You’ll Love It

- Serves Illy Italian coffee
- Convenient Galleria location
- Traditional Italian gelato flavors
- Crepes, paninis, and waffle cones
- Longstanding Uptown café presence

## The Coffee

The main reason for stopping by? A warm, comforting **caffè latte made with Illy espresso**.

Illy remains one of my favorite Italian coffee brands, known for its balanced flavor and smooth finish. The latte delivered exactly what was needed — warmth, creamy texture, and a smooth, delicate espresso profile without excessive bitterness.

When strolling through The Galleria, quality coffee options can feel limited. Cafe Dolce consistently fills that gap.

## The Gelato

Although not initially in the mood for dessert, sampling the gelato revealed some inconsistency. The texture appeared dry and stiff rather than creamy and freshly churned.

Traditionally, Italian gelato should be silky, dense, and smooth — and in the past, this spot delivered exactly that. During this visit, however, the display cases looked slightly lackluster.

On the bright side, many of the flavors remain traditional and authentically Italian. Notably, the pistachios are sourced directly from Sicily — a promising detail for purists.

Fresh waffle cones are also made in-house to accompany gelato orders.

## Savory Options

Beyond sweets, Cafe Dolce also offers:

- 🥪 Caprese panini
- 🥞 Crepes
- 🍦 Fresh waffle cones

The caprese panini, tried previously, was satisfying and makes for a light lunch option during a shopping day.

## The Atmosphere

As a longtime tenant in The Galleria, Cafe Dolce Gelato maintains a charming European café feel. It’s a reliable stop when navigating the shopping crowds — a place to recharge with espresso and rest your feet.

## Overall Impression

Cafe Dolce Gelato remains a dependable **Italian coffee stop inside The Galleria**, particularly for fans of Illy espresso. While gelato consistency may vary, the café still serves as a convenient retreat for lattes, crepes, and light bites during a busy shopping day.

For now, the coffee continues to be the highlight — smooth, comforting, and reliably Italian.
