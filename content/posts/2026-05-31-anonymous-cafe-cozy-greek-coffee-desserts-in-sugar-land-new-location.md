## The Atmosphere

Anonymous Café is small and quaint — just a few tables, chairs, and even a cozy sofa chair to sink into. The limited seating adds to its charm, creating a homey, intimate vibe that feels welcoming and relaxed.

It’s the kind of place where you can linger with a coffee and dessert without feeling rushed.

## The Coffee

### ☕ Greek Iced Coffee

Strong. Bold. Bitter.

If you’re not accustomed to robust coffee, this might not be your first choice. I always request extra milk to soften the intensity. One tip: ask for less ice and more coffee to get the most out of your drink.

### ☕ Lattes

For those who prefer a smoother option, their lattes are well-balanced and enjoyable — a safer pick if you’re easing into Greek-style coffee.

## The Desserts (Must-Try)

One of the standout reasons to visit Anonymous Café is the dessert selection.

### 🍯 Honey & Nut Cookies

Fragrant, syrupy, and beautifully paired with strong coffee.

### 🍰 Eckmeck

A layered Greek dessert made with:

- Shredded phyllo
- Custard
- Cream
- Toasted almonds
- Powdered sugar

Heavenly and indulgent — yet not overwhelmingly sweet. A true highlight.

Pound cakes are also available and look tempting for a future visit.

## Savory Options

The café also offers savory Greek-inspired dishes, which appear fresh and homemade. While I haven’t tried them yet, Mediterranean cuisine is known for its wholesome and healthy ingredients — making it an appealing option for next time.

## Service

The owners and staff are friendly and genuinely welcoming. You feel like a regular, even on your first visit — a rare quality in many coffee shops.
