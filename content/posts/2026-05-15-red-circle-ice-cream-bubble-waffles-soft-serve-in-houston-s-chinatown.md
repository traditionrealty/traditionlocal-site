Located in **Houston’s Chinatown along Bellaire Boulevard**, Red Circle Ice Cream brings a trendy West Coast dessert concept to the city. Known for its **customizable bubble waffles, churros, and soft serve creations**, this late-night dessert spot adds a playful twist to Houston’s already vibrant sweets scene.

Popularized in cities like Los Angeles, the bubble waffle-and-ice-cream combination has become a social-media favorite — crispy golden waffles folded into cones and stuffed with swirls of soft serve and toppings. Red Circle delivers that same customizable experience right here in Houston.

## The Dessert Experience

After dinner in Chinatown, this was the perfect next stop for something sweet. The menu is straightforward and easy to navigate — ideal when you already know you’re craving soft serve.

Red Circle specializes in:

- Bubble waffle cones
- Churro-based sundaes
- Classic soft serve
- Custom toppings

It’s an interactive dessert experience that lets you build your ideal sweet treat.

## What We Ordered

### 🍦 Frosty Churro (Vanilla Soft Serve + Cinnamon Churros)

The Frosty Churro featured vanilla soft serve paired with two warm cinnamon churros.

The soft serve was creamy and smooth, though noticeably sweet — slightly sweeter than preferred. While enjoyable, it leaned heavily into sugary territory. Texture-wise, it was consistent and velvety.

The churros, however, stood out. Warm, crisp, and well-textured, they complemented the soft serve beautifully. Their crunch balanced the creaminess, making each bite more satisfying.

You can also order churros separately — which might be the move next time.

### 🧇 Bubble Waffle (Taste Test)

A bite of a friend’s bubble waffle revealed a crisp texture. While some prefer softer, eggier waffles, the crispness here helps structurally support the ice cream and toppings — practical and functional for loaded dessert builds.

## Customization Options

There are several additional toppings available, though restraint may be wise — especially if you’re sensitive to sweetness. The base dessert already delivers a generous sugar hit.

## Service & Atmosphere

Red Circle offers strong customer service and maintains a lively, social atmosphere. With late closing hours, it’s a convenient after-dinner dessert stop for friends and families exploring Chinatown.
