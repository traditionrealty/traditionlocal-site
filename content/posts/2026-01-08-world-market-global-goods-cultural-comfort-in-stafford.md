World Market in Stafford offers a vibrant, culturally rich escape—perfect for anyone who thrives on global flavors, décor, and eclectic treasures. Though smaller than the long-established Post Oak location, this newer store still brings an impressive spread of international goods, pantry staples, and uniquely crafted home items. From European chocolates to Middle Eastern spices, from artisan mugs to bright textiles, the store feels like a tiny world tour tucked inside the suburbs.

Service is warm, the ambiance is cozy, and the aisles are full of delightful surprises—halal gummy candies, curated teas, candles, cookware, pottery, and seasonal imports that keep each visit fresh. For anyone craving the joy of travel during quiet seasons or limited movement, this shop becomes an unexpected comfort.

### **Must-Try Picks**

- German marzipan chocolate
- British & international teas
- Halal gummy candies
- Global spices & pantry goods
- Candles, pottery, and unique ceramic bowls

### **Review**

**Sara I. (Jul 21, 2020):** 

“It is wonderful to explore this culturally diverse store during limited moments of traveling. Anything cultural and world related excites the heck out of me. Thank y’all for opening this store during such obscure times.

World Market has always been a favorite of mine—we had a Sugar Land location over a decade ago, where I’d grab all my culinary staples. It closed long ago, so having one in Stafford now, just minutes from Sugar Land, is such a treat.

This location feels smaller than the Post Oak one, but there’s still so much to browse: candles, spices, halal gummy candies, chocolate (the German marzipan is my favorite!), and a lovely selection of British and international teas. The mugs, bowls, and pottery are beautiful as well.

If you’re into cultural and global goods like I am, you’ll dig this store. It’s my home away from home. Good service and warm ambiance. When the travel bug bites, I make my way to World Market. Until next time. Adeus!”
