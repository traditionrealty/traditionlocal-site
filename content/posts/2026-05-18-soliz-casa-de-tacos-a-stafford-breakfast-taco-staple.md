Located in **Stafford, TX**, Soliz Casa De Tacos has long been known for its **breakfast tacos, barbacoa, menudo, and classic Tex-Mex plates**. A neighborhood name that’s been around for decades, it draws loyal regulars looking for hearty morning tacos and traditional Mexican comfort food.

With a newly renovated and clean interior, Soliz presents itself as a casual breakfast and brunch stop. The menu is fairly extensive, featuring combination plates, breakfast taco varieties, sides, and drinks — with breakfast tacos being the clear crowd favorite.

## The Visit

Arriving about 30 minutes before closing for a light lunch, the experience felt a bit rushed. Service was polite but slightly awkward, with noticeably warmer interactions toward regular customers.

The interior, however, was clean and recently renovated — offering a refreshed look for a longstanding establishment.

## What We Ordered

### 🌮 The “Eric” Breakfast Taco

This taco included eggs, potatoes, onions, cheese, and guacamole — a combination that should deliver balanced flavor and texture.

In reality, the taco leaned heavily on potatoes, creating a starchy, one-note bite. The eggs — typically the star of any breakfast taco — were sparse and scattered, lacking the fluffy presence expected. The overall flavor felt muted, with minimal seasoning coming through.

The tortilla carried a slightly smoky taste that felt off-balance rather than complementary.

### 🌶 Salsa

The salsa had decent flavor and ended up doing much of the heavy lifting — adding needed moisture and taste to an otherwise underwhelming taco.

## Menu Notes

- Breakfast tacos are the most popular draw.
- Beans are cooked with pork and are **not vegetarian-friendly**.
- Combination plates and traditional items like barbacoa and menudo are available.

## Overall Impression

Soliz Casa De Tacos benefits from its longstanding reputation and updated interior, but this particular visit did not deliver the flavor or balance expected from a well-known breakfast taco spot.

With strong competition nearby — including other taco joints on the same street — flavor, portion balance, and consistency are key differentiators in Stafford’s Tex-Mex scene.

While Soliz may remain a favorite among longtime regulars, first-time visitors may want to explore multiple local options before deciding on their go-to breakfast taco destination.
