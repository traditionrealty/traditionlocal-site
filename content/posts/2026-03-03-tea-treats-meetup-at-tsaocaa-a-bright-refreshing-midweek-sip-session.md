The **Tea & Treats Meetup at TSAOCAA** brought together vibrant drinks, light bites, and joyful conversation in a bright, welcoming café setting. With its airy interior, plenty of seating, and laid-back energy, TSAOCAA proved to be the perfect environment for a midweek reset—equal parts refreshing and fun.

Guests sampled fruity, floral, and chewy tea creations that showed off the shop’s diverse menu. From nata jelly–filled sips to tapioca-studded drinks, the offerings were beautifully crafted, satisfying, and ideal for a warm Houston afternoon. The standout? A pineapple jasmine tea with nata jelly—an aromatic, perfumy blend that was both sensual and incredibly refreshing.

**Event Highlights:**

- Airy, inviting café atmosphere with plentiful seating
- Pineapple jasmine tea with nata jelly (the star of the afternoon!)
- Tapioca pearl tea with delicious chew
- Crunchy, savory nuggets for snacking
- Warm conversation, community connections & cozy vibes
- Fun trinkets and small goodies shared around the table

## **Review**

Sara I. (May 20, 2024):

“Juicy brewin' tea drinks and crunchy lil' nuggets? My type of hour right here! Exclusive convos with the homie Jando? Yes please! I love office hours and this one was a real treat.

This spot is cute, light and airy. Plenty of seating @ Tsaocaa without it being too big or too small of a space. The vibes were immaculate. Just right.

I'm lovin' their tea menu here. What a neat spot! Happy they opened up a location in the HOU. Tried two different beverages and both were refreshing. The first had nata jelly (made with coconut pulp and pineapple flavoring) & the second drink consisted of tapioca pearls. Both were chewy and tantalizing, but I must say, the pineapple with jasmine tea and nata jelly were totally delightful. The floral notes of jasmine were quite aromatic and sensual. Perfumy without being overwhelming. Probably the best I've had.

Thanks for the complimentary tea(s)! As well as the loot, cute little trinkets scattered round' the table. The pizza cutter will come in clutch as well as the travel accessory bag. Always appreciated.

It was a tasty Tuesday tea day. Spent with good chatter, caffeine and fruity chews. We also had the youngest attendee join in on all the fun. The bebe was all giggles and smiles. What a delight. Until next time!”
