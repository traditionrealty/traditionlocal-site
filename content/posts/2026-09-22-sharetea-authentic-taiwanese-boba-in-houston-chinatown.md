If you are craving authentic Taiwanese style tapioca drinks in Houston, Sharetea in Chinatown is a must visit. Since boba originated in Taiwan, it is reassuring to step into a place that stays true to that concept.

This bright and lively café is small but thoughtfully arranged with plenty of tables and chairs. It is a popular gathering spot for students, friends, and anyone looking to enjoy a refreshing milk tea.

If you are searching for:

- Best boba in Houston Chinatown
- Taiwanese milk tea Houston
- Honey milk tea Houston
- Study spots in Chinatown Houston

Sharetea is a strong contender.

## Why You Will Love It

- Authentic Taiwanese style drinks
- Customizable sweetness and ice levels
- Reliable WiFi connection
- Bright and casual atmosphere
- Wide variety of milk teas and fruit drinks

## The Atmosphere

The café is brightly lit and often buzzing with energy. There is WiFi and a strong internet connection, making it a convenient place to study, read, or work online. It can get a little loud during peak hours, but for light tasks or casual browsing, it works well.

It is almost always packed with long lines, which speaks to its popularity. On a rainy Sunday evening, however, the crowd was manageable and service was quick.

## What I Ordered

### Honey Milk Tea with Tapioca

Highly recommended.

The drink was sweet, crisp, and refreshing with the perfect amount of tapioca and ice. The pearls had a great texture and were not overly chewy. Next time, I would likely request slightly less sweetness, but overall it was very satisfying.

I am also looking forward to trying the Okinawa milk tea, which is sweetened with roasted brown sugar and known for its rich flavor.

## Overall Impression

Sharetea delivers authentic Taiwanese boba drinks in the heart of Houston’s Chinatown. It is a dependable spot when you are craving milk tea, and it remains one of the more popular choices in the area.

Whether you are stopping in to study or simply grabbing a drink to go, it is worth the visit.

Sharetea equals shared happiness.
